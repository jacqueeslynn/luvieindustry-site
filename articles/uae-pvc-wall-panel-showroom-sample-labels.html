PVC wall panel samples for UAE showrooms: make every selection traceable

A visitor selects a warm wood-look panel in a UAE showroom, then sends a photograph to the purchasing team. The photograph shows the face but not the model, finish code or profile. Several similar products may fit that description. Before an importer expands its display, it is worth deciding how each sample will lead to an identifiable quotation. A clear label is a practical sales tool: it connects a physical selection to the product information the supplier must confirm.

Short answer

Give each sample a stable internal identifier and connect it to the supplier model, finish reference and current specification record. Separate confirmed information from fields awaiting verification. Keep the identifier visible in sample photographs and carry it into the inquiry. A sample label is not a certificate, stock confirmation or promise of unchanged future supply.

Independent reference

GS1: product identifiers and barcodes. General guidance, not a Luvie product certification.

Identify the physical sample, not only the collection

Start with a unique internal sample reference that staff can read and type. Record the supplier model and finish reference separately; an attractive collection name alone may describe several profiles. Where available, include the date received and the supplier's sample reference. A cut sample may not represent the full panel width, so distinguish the sample size from the dimensions of the saleable product. Do not guess missing dimensions from a photograph. Keep the sample reference on the reverse as well as on the display card so the object remains identifiable when removed from its stand.

Separate identification from performance information

GS1 distinguishes a product identifier from the barcode that carries data. That distinction is useful when planning a sample system: a scannable code should lead to a maintained record, not replace it. This guide does not require a GS1 identifier for a showroom sample or imply that Luvie supplies one. A local sample number can serve the internal workflow. Avoid printing unsupported waterproof, fire-rated or exterior-use claims on the card. If a specification is pending, say that it needs confirmation rather than borrowing information from a visually similar panel. The person opening the record should see which facts are verified for the exact offered model.

Make photographs and digital records agree

Place the sample identifier where it can appear beside the face in a selection photograph. Add a reverse-side photograph when the connection geometry matters, without implying that an image establishes installed coverage. Maintain one record containing the identifier, supplier reference, approved images and the latest document version. If a QR code is used, test it on an ordinary phone and retain a readable identifier for people who cannot scan it. Keep customer details out of publicly accessible records. A broken link should not make a sample untraceable, and replacing a digital image should not silently change the product associated with an existing approval.

Carry the selection into the quotation

Ask the buyer to include the sample reference, intended interior location and approximate quantity in the inquiry. The quotation should name the offered model and finish, with current dimensions, packaging and order terms confirmed separately. If the supplier proposes an alternative, provide a new identified sample and obtain fresh approval rather than keeping the old display card. For a UAE showroom serving several languages, retain the same model and sample codes across translated labels. Translate the explanatory words, not the identifier itself. This keeps sales staff, purchasing and the customer discussing the same selection without assuming that a display guarantees inventory or delivery timing.

Before you request a quotation

  • Stable sample identifier on the object and display card.
  • Supplier model and finish references recorded separately.
  • Confirmed facts distinguished from pending information.
  • Quotation linked to the selected sample and current offer.

Frequently asked questions

Does every showroom sample need a barcode?

No. A readable stable identifier and a maintained record may be sufficient. A barcode or QR code is an optional way to retrieve that record.

Can the same sample approval cover a replacement model?

Do not assume so. Identify the replacement and ask the buyer to review the relevant appearance and specification differences before accepting it.
