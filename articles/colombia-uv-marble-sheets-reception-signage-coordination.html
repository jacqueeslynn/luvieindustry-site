Colombia Reception Walls: Coordinate Sheets and Signs

A reception wall often combines a decorative marble-look surface with a company sign and carefully placed lighting. For a Colombian office project, ordering UV sheets before the sign layout is resolved can leave visible holes, awkward joints or inaccessible wiring. The buyer should bring the sign fabricator, wall installer and electrical professional into the same detail review.

Short answer

Approve the complete elevation and support arrangement before fabrication. Keep the decorative sheet separate from sign loads and electrical design, and reserve access for components that may need maintenance.

Independent reference

NIST: SI units. General guidance, not a Luvie product certification.

Freeze the sign layout and sheet layout together

Place the sign outline, letter positions, visible joints and sheet direction on one elevation. Ask the fabricator which dimensions are final and which depend on a site survey. Use consistent units and a clearly identified drawing revision; NIST’s SI reference supports measurement consistency but does not set fabrication tolerances. A decorative pattern can make a joint more conspicuous near a letter or logo. Review the proposed layout at useful scale rather than approving a small sample and expecting the installer to invent the composition.

Specify support independently of the decorative face

The sign fabricator and responsible designer should determine how loads are transferred to suitable supporting construction. Do not assume a thin decorative sheet can hold signage or standoffs by itself. Coordinate holes, spacers and the finished build-up with the panel supplier’s guidance. If letters are bonded rather than mechanically fixed, request compatibility information for the actual materials and conditions. A successful visual mock-up is not structural approval, and a product photograph cannot establish that a proposed attachment method is suitable.

Plan electrical and service access

For illuminated signage, have the qualified electrical professional define wiring, equipment placement and access requirements under applicable local rules. Do not permanently conceal components that require inspection or replacement simply to keep the wall visually uninterrupted. Confirm whether removable parts can be accessed without damaging surrounding sheets. Keep any required ventilation or separation details visible in the coordinated drawing. These are project responsibilities, not questions that can be settled by a generic statement that UV marble-look sheets are suitable for offices.

Approve appearance and a repair route

Review the finish with the intended lighting and an appropriate sign sample where practical. Check reflections, visible edges and the junction around any service panel. Agree how a damaged sheet or changed sign would be managed later, including the limitations of matching replacement material. Calculate quantities from the approved layout and include the required edge components separately. This gives the Colombian buyer a complete procurement scope while avoiding promises of invisible joints, unlimited adhesive compatibility or effortless future rebranding.

Before you request a quotation

  • Coordinate sign and sheet layouts on one revised drawing.
  • Verify sign support independently of cladding.
  • Maintain access to electrical components.
  • Review lighting, edges and future replacement needs.

Frequently asked questions

Can lettering be fixed directly to any decorative sheet?

No. The attachment method and supporting construction must be checked for the exact materials and loads.

Can the electrical driver simply be hidden behind a sheet?

The electrical professional must define a compliant, maintainable arrangement; decorative appearance does not override access requirements.
