SPC flooring for Peru: agree how thickness will be measured before inspection

Two photographs of a digital gauge can show different readings without explaining whether the same dimension was measured. One operator may include the attached backing while another contacts a different part of the profile. For a Peruvian importer preparing an SPC order, the useful question is therefore not just what number should appear. It is how the buyer and supplier will produce comparable evidence. This guide separates a purchasing inspection agreement from a laboratory method and does not set a Luvie thickness tolerance or claim that an ordinary caliper measures a wear layer.

Short answer

Define the dimension, product condition and measurement positions before selecting the instrument and acceptance rule. Ask a competent measurement specialist to confirm an appropriate method for the agreed specification. Record the instrument identity and status alongside readings, and agree how conflicting results will be investigated. More decimal places on a display do not by themselves establish a more accurate measurement.

Define the dimension before discussing a tolerance

State whether the enquiry concerns the complete plank, an explicitly defined construction without backing or another specified feature. Connect that definition to the current product drawing or written specification. Do not infer individual layer thickness from an external measurement across the assembled plank. Ask the supplier to identify suitable contact locations and any areas that should not be used, including profile features that do not represent the agreed dimension. Keep the sample condition recorded. Do not peel, compress, cut or otherwise modify the material for a receiving check unless the agreed method calls for it. A nominal catalogue value and a purchasing acceptance limit are different items; obtain the applicable agreement rather than inventing a tolerance.

Select the instrument and technique for that dimension

Ask the responsible quality professional whether the proposed instrument and contact geometry are appropriate for the material and required decision. Mitutoyo's measuring-instrument guidance identifies measuring force and temperature differences among factors that can affect caliper readings. That is general metrology background, not an SPC test method or Luvie acceptance rule. Follow the selected method for handling, alignment, force and conditions instead of squeezing until the displayed value looks familiar. Record the instrument identifier and relevant calibration or verification information. A zero check is useful only within the applicable procedure; it does not replace the wider assessment of whether the instrument is suitable for the task.

Make the readings reproducible rather than photogenic

Agree who selects the planks, how many are reviewed and which positions are recorded, with the sampling plan defined by the responsible parties. Do not invent a universal sample count. Link each reading to a plank identifier and location, and keep the raw observations rather than reporting only a favourable average. A photograph should show enough context to understand the contact position and units, while the written record captures information that the image cannot establish. If two operators disagree, compare the dimension definition, instrument status, position and technique first. Repeatedly measuring different locations until one reading passes is not a defensible resolution of the disagreement.

Agree what a discrepancy triggers before goods arrive

Define who reviews an unexpected result and whether the affected material remains on hold while the measurement is checked. Preserve the sample identity and original record; do not destroy the disputed feature during an unplanned follow-up test. If a specialist or laboratory must resolve the issue, agree the question and method before sending material. Record the final decision and its scope without presenting an informal receiving check as product certification. Feed any clarified measurement instructions into the next purchase order so the same disagreement is not repeated. Thickness acceptance also does not establish all other flooring properties, installation suitability or performance under the intended use.

Before you request a quotation

  • Dimension and included layers defined in writing.
  • Instrument and method reviewed for the required decision.
  • Plank identifiers, positions, units and raw readings recorded.
  • Discrepancy owner, hold status and escalation agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Can a caliper reading confirm the wear-layer specification?

An overall external measurement does not isolate the wear layer. Ask what method and evidence are appropriate for that specific property.

Does checking zero prove that the tool is calibrated?

No. Record and review the calibration or verification status required by the agreed procedure. A zero check alone does not establish suitability across the measurement task.
