UAE SPC flooring procurement: compare indentation evidence, not just small numbers

An SPC offer showing a smaller indentation number can look better until the buyer notices a different method, unit or reporting symbol. For a UAE fit-out quotation, preserve those details before ranking products. This guide uses one external product's published results to demonstrate the reading method, not to recommend it or assign its performance to Luvie. The Polyflor Expona EnCore Rigid Loc PUR page was checked on 25 November 2026.

Short answer

Polyflor reports less than 0.10 mm under ISO 24343-1, and no more than 0.005 inch under two separately named ASTM entries. The latter converts to 0.127 mm, but conversion does not make the methods equivalent. A stated limit is not an exact measured result and cannot support a precise performance ratio.

Keep the symbol attached to the number

The difference between < and ≤ is meaningful: one excludes the stated boundary and the other includes it. More importantly, neither tells you the exact measured indentation. In the illustrative row, A could have a result near its limit while B could have a much smaller result. Their declarations alone therefore do not establish which specimen performed better. Ask whether the quotation gives an individual measurement, an acceptance limit or a manufacturer declaration, and retain the original wording in the comparison sheet.

Normalize units without erasing the method

Multiplying inches by 25.4 is a unit conversion; it does not recreate a test. The manufacturer lists ASTM F1914 separately from ASTM F970 (Modified), and identifies 750 psi only in the modified entry. Do not attach that pressure to the ISO row or drop the word Modified. Ask for the report, loading arrangement, duration, recovery period and specimen construction needed to establish comparability. This article does not reconstruct those conditions from the summary table.

Turn the project question into a document request

For the UAE project, list fixed furniture, moving furniture, castors and any unusually concentrated contact points. Send their relevant loading and contact details to the flooring supplier and the responsible project professional. A laboratory residual-indentation declaration is not a blanket approval for every furniture arrangement. Request the selected model's installation and protection instructions, including compatibility with the intended substrate and backing. Do not infer approval from the core name, total thickness or a photograph of a completed room.

Approve the offered model rather than the reference example

Record the exact model, document revision and construction covered by each report. If two offers lack matched evidence, mark the performance comparison unresolved instead of inventing a common score. Keep dimensional checks, wear-layer evidence and indentation evidence in separate columns. The named Polyflor results here belong only to that external reference; Luvie's offered product requires its own current documents. Final selection should reflect the project's complete requirements, not the smallest isolated number.

Before you request a quotation

  • Retain < or ≤, units, method and any Modified notation.
  • Distinguish measured results from limits or declarations.
  • Request the exact offered model's report and assembly description.
  • Provide furniture contact/loading details and confirm protection requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Is 0.005 inch less than 0.10 mm?

No: 0.005 inch equals 0.127 mm. However, these entries use different methods, so that arithmetic does not rank performance.

Does a thicker wear layer prevent indentation?

The thickness alone cannot establish that outcome. Obtain relevant indentation evidence for the complete selected construction and intended use.
