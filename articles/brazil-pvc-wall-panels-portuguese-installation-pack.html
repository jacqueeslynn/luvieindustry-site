PVC wall panels for Brazil: prepare a Portuguese installation pack

A Brazilian distributor may receive the right PVC wall panels but still lose time answering the same installer questions. A catalog shows finishes; it does not necessarily explain how a particular panel meets a corner, an opening or a wet-area junction. Before printing a Portuguese leaflet, build a controlled handover pack for the exact product being ordered. This is a practical documentation workflow, not a statement of Brazilian regulatory compliance.

Short answer

Keep the approved product code, current installation instructions, accessory schedule and Portuguese terminology together. Have the supplier confirm technical meaning and a local installer check whether the instructions can actually be followed.

Separate the catalog from installation instructions

Start with a document register: product reference, supplier instruction revision, Portuguese revision, approval date and responsible reviewer. Identify whether the order is for wall or ceiling use and which applications remain unapproved. A photograph of a similar profile is not a substitute for the ordered drawing. If instructions are missing, request them before translating; do not fill the gap with another manufacturer’s fixing spacing or adhesive recommendation.

Translate the decisions, not only the headings

Agree terms such as largura útil, perfil de acabamento and junta de dilatação with the Brazilian installation team. Show dimensions in clearly identified units, explain coverage separately from overall panel width and preserve every qualification in the original instructions. Include a labeled sketch of the actual trim assembly. Where a supplier drawing is unclear, send a question back instead of letting the translator invent a construction detail.

Make water-related limitations visible

The linked Dumaplast guidance discusses sealing joints for its own wet-room installations. It illustrates why panel material and installed joints are separate questions; it does not approve a Luvie system. Ask for the ordered panel’s permitted locations, substrate preparation, compatible joint products and treatment around penetrations. Do not turn a general waterproof headline into permission for every shower assembly. Have the project team resolve missing wet-area details before purchase.

Trial the pack before releasing it to dealers

Give the pack and a small mockup kit to an installer who did not write it. Ask them to identify the first panel, finishing trim, restrictions and whom to contact when the wall differs from the drawing. Record unanswered questions and revise the pack. Put a revision identifier on the PDF and printed sheet; keep a stable download link and withdraw superseded copies. For each shipment, record which revision accompanied the product.

Before you request a quotation

  • Exact panel reference and approved application
  • Supplier instructions and Portuguese revision register
  • Matched trims and unresolved technical questions
  • Installer trial and controlled download link

Frequently asked questions

Can the product catalog serve as the installation manual?

Only if it actually contains the approved instructions for that exact system. A finish catalog alone is insufficient.

Must the original product PDF be rewritten?

No. Keep it unchanged and supply a separate Portuguese introduction and approved installation pack.
