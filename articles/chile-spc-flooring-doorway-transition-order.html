SPC flooring for Chile: plan doorway transitions before ordering

A floor quotation can look complete until the installer reaches the first doorway. For a project in Chile, ordering the boards and resolving every threshold later can leave a small accessory holding up a large room.

Short answer

Make a doorway schedule before ordering SPC flooring. For every opening, record adjacent floor finishes, finished heights, door clearance and the proposed profile. Confirm movement requirements with the selected product’s installation guide.

Survey each threshold, not just the floor area

Number the openings on the plan and photograph each one from both sides. Record whether the floor meets tile, another floating floor or an unfinished space. Measure the expected finished levels rather than the bare substrates alone. An installer should review the door swing and clearance after all layers are included; a matching colour does not make a transition mechanically suitable.

Separate appearance from movement requirements

An uninterrupted surface may be the design preference, but allowable continuous runs and doorway details depend on the selected system. The referenced Karndean guide gives conditions for its own rigid-core products. It is a useful example of why installation documents matter, not a source of universal joint dimensions for Luvie. Ask for the applicable detail before promising a seamless layout.

Buy profiles as a coordinated package

Ask for profile type, usable length, finish, fixing method and compatibility with the planned height difference. Show which edge will remain visible. Count cuts and short returns separately; purchasing only the net doorway width can leave insufficient usable lengths. Confirm that fixing a profile will not restrain a floating floor contrary to its instructions. Obtain the installer’s approval of the complete detail.

Resolve site substitutions before dispatch

For a Chile-bound shipment, identify which accessories will arrive with the flooring and which will be sourced locally. If a substitute is proposed, approve its section and finish, not just its name. Keep a spare strategy for unusual thresholds and label packages against the doorway schedule. Photograph the first completed opening before repeating the detail across the project.

Before you request a quotation

  • Doorway schedule with photographs and finished floor levels.
  • Selected SPC system and current installation instructions.
  • Profile sections, finish references and quantities by length.
  • Installer approval and local-versus-imported accessory scope.

Frequently asked questions

Does every doorway need the same trim?

No. Equal-height joints, height changes and exposed edges can need different details. Confirm each location.

Can a trim hide an incorrectly sized movement gap?

A cover profile is not a substitute for the product’s required movement arrangement. Resolve the joint before installation.
