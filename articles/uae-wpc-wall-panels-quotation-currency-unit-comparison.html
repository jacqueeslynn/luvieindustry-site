UAE WPC wall panels: compare quotations on one declared basis

One WPC quotation may be priced per panel in one currency while another is per carton in another currency. A third may quote square metres without explaining whether that means nominal face area or effective installed coverage. The lowest visible number is therefore not necessarily the lowest comparable offer. A UAE purchasing team needs a controlled comparison sheet that preserves each original quotation while making every conversion assumption visible to the people approving the order.

Short answer

Confirm the exact reference and commercial scope first, then normalize quantities and currency using documented inputs. Keep product price, accessories, packing and delivery costs in separate rows. Mark missing amounts as unresolved instead of zero, and distinguish an internal comparison estimate from the supplier amount that will actually be invoiced.

Define a genuinely comparable product quantity

Record the supplier reference, dimensions, pieces per carton and quoted selling unit. Ask how usable coverage is calculated for the selected profile and whether the quoted area uses the same definition. Do not assume the visible width equals effective installed width. Choose a common comparison quantity tied to the project requirement, then show the conversion from that quantity to whole panels and cartons. Keep rounding visible because an order cannot always buy a fraction of a sealed carton. Separate cutting allowance from manufacturer coverage; it is a project-planning assumption, not a way to inflate the product area. If two designs require different accessories, carry those differences into the comparison.

Declare the exchange-rate assumption and its date

Keep the quoted currency and original amount unchanged in the source columns. In the comparison columns, record the chosen reporting currency, conversion source, rate direction and date. A rate expressed as reporting-currency units per supplier-currency unit uses a different calculation from its inverse, so write the direction in words. Treat this as a purchasing comparison, not a guarantee of the settlement rate. Ask the finance team which fees or payment arrangements need separate consideration and record them only when known. If a quotation expires before the planned decision, request confirmation rather than carrying forward its price as though validity were unlimited.

Align delivery scope before adding totals

ICC explains that Incoterms rules clarify tasks, costs and risks in delivery between sellers and buyers. Record the exact agreed rule, named place or port and edition for each quotation, and ask the responsible parties to clarify any incomplete wording. A price associated with one delivery point should not be compared directly with a price covering a different scope. Keep freight, handling, insurance where relevant, and importer-assessed charges as separately identified inputs supported by current information. This guide does not calculate UAE taxes or determine customs treatment. Do not label a subtotal as a complete landed cost while necessary charges or responsibilities remain unresolved.

Keep the decision traceable when assumptions change

Save the supplier quotations alongside the comparison revision and date. Identify which cells are supplier-confirmed facts, which are project assumptions and which remain open. Recalculate when packing quantities, coverage, currency assumptions or delivery scope change; do not edit only the grand total. Ask a second reviewer to reproduce one conversion independently and compare it with the worksheet result. The purchasing decision should also consider specification fit and supporting evidence, not merely price ranking. Before issuing the order, confirm the final product quantity, invoicing currency, payment terms and delivery wording with the supplier. Archive the accepted version so later invoice checks use the agreed basis.

Before you request a quotation

  • Exact product, selling unit and effective coverage confirmed.
  • Original currency retained and conversion direction/date documented.
  • Delivery rule, named location and excluded costs made visible.
  • Final order checked against the accepted comparison revision.

Frequently asked questions

Can a per-square-metre quote be compared directly with a per-panel quote?

Only after confirming the selected panel coverage definition and the quantity conversion. Include whole-carton rounding and relevant accessories separately.

Is the converted comparison total the amount the bank will debit?

Not necessarily. It is an estimate based on stated assumptions. Settlement rates, fees and agreed payment arrangements require separate confirmation with the responsible finance parties.
