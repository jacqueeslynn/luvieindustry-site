Brazil PVC wall panels: migrate an obsolete SKU without rewriting order history

A distributor can retire a PVC wall-panel range without losing the records needed to serve previous buyers. The difficult moment comes when a sales system treats the replacement as the same item: old invoices suddenly display new dimensions, a customer receives a different joining profile, or a return is booked against stock that never supplied the order. For Brazilian distributors, a controlled SKU migration is an operational handover between two product records, not simply a new photograph on an existing listing.

Short answer

Keep the discontinued reference and its historical specifications intact. Create a separately identified replacement when appropriate, connect the records with an explicit relationship, and review open orders before redirecting new enquiries. Do not describe a successor as interchangeable until the relevant dimensions, profile, finish and application have been checked.

Separate discontinuation from deletion

Record why the old reference is being retired and who confirmed the supplier status. Distinguish permanent discontinuation from a temporary supply interruption or a local decision to stop stocking the item. Preserve the original supplier code, local SKU, approved specification, selling unit and images with their revision dates. Historical invoices and dispatch records should continue identifying what the customer actually bought. GS1 guidance on GTIN non-reuse reinforces the importance of preserving product identity, but an internal warehouse SKU is not automatically a GTIN. If your range uses GS1 identifiers, apply the relevant GS1 allocation rules rather than transferring a retired number to a different product for convenience.

Build a successor comparison before recommending it

Create a side-by-side review of the old and proposed new reference. Confirm dimensions, effective coverage, joining geometry, compatible trims, finish identification and the intended application using current supplier records. A matching marketing name is not evidence that two profiles can be joined in one installation. Where a detail is unknown, label it as an open question rather than filling the cell with the earlier specification. Decide whether the relationship means equivalent for a defined use, alternative requiring approval, or simply another option. Record the person who approved that description and the evidence reviewed. Do not promise that a new production finish will invisibly match an aged installation.

Resolve existing demand before changing the storefront

List unfulfilled orders, outstanding quotations, reserved cartons, samples with customers and expected returns. Contact the responsible account owner to decide whether each commitment can still be fulfilled as quoted or needs a documented change accepted by the buyer. Keep remaining old stock visible to authorized staff without implying unlimited replenishment. On the Portuguese product page, explain the actual availability status and provide a clearly described alternative where suitable. Do not silently redirect a detailed old product page to a different model while retaining the old technical claims. Review downloadable specifications and sales messages alongside the online listing so contradictory versions do not remain in circulation.

Test the migration with real operational scenarios

Before closing the change, ask staff to look up an old invoice, process a simulated return and prepare a new quotation for the successor. Each task should preserve the correct product identity and selling unit. Check that a barcode scan cannot allocate replacement stock to an order requiring the discontinued item without an approved change. Assign an owner for obsolete samples and printed sales sheets; mark or withdraw them from active selling while retaining necessary archives. Set a review date for residual stock and unresolved customer commitments. The result should be a clear commercial transition, not a cleaner catalog that hides the information needed for service and accountability.

Before you request a quotation

  • Old product identity and historical specification preserved.
  • Successor relationship supported by a documented technical comparison.
  • Open orders, quotations, returns and remaining stock reviewed.
  • Portuguese listings and operational test cases checked together.

Frequently asked questions

Can we reuse the old stock code for a similar new panel?

Avoid overwriting history. Use a separate record where needed and an explicit successor link. If a GTIN is involved, follow GS1 allocation and non-reuse rules rather than treating it as an arbitrary internal code.

Should every discontinued page disappear immediately?

Not necessarily. Existing customers may need its specifications. Keep useful historical information clearly marked and distinguish it from the offer currently available for new orders.
