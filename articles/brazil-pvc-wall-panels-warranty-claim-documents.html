PVC wall panels in Brazil: build a clear warranty and claim file

A long warranty headline is difficult to use if the buyer cannot identify the supplied batch or understand the exclusions. Brazilian distributors should build a practical claim file before the first order, not after a customer reports a problem.

Short answer

Obtain the written warranty for the exact SKU and intended use, identify the party handling claims, and preserve order, batch and installation records. Separate manufacturing concerns from transport damage and installation issues without deciding liability prematurely.

Compare the scope behind the headline

Ask what is covered, what is excluded, when coverage starts and what remedy is offered. Clarify the evidence and reporting procedure, including any contractual deadlines. Do not assume that replacement material includes removal, labour or onward transport. Have the relevant commercial or legal adviser review obligations applicable to your Brazilian resale arrangement; this guide does not replace that advice.

Keep product identity attached to the sale

Record the SKU, finish, batch or lot reference, purchase order and delivery record. GS1’s traceability standard explains the value of distinguishing batches, but it does not establish a warranty or require that Luvie uses a particular barcode system. The practical aim is to connect the reported issue to the actual supplied material rather than to a generic catalogue photograph.

Create an evidence checklist before a complaint

Prepare a simple Portuguese intake form for photographs, affected quantity, location, date observed and installation details. Preserve packaging labels and an unused sample where available. Ask staff not to discard evidence or promise a remedy before review. Keep customer contact details in the business’s controlled support channel rather than a public article comment or shared image folder.

Make the response route visible

Identify one distributor contact and the supplier escalation route. Record requests and replies against the same case reference. Distinguish an acknowledgement from acceptance of liability, and explain what information is still needed. Reviewing a sample claim file before launch helps the team discover missing documents without waiting for a real dispute.

Before you request a quotation

  • Exact warranty document, SKU and intended-use scope.
  • Batch, order and delivery references retained together.
  • Portuguese evidence intake form and secure case record.
  • Named response route and clarified remedy exclusions.

Frequently asked questions

Is the longest warranty automatically the strongest offer?

No. Coverage, exclusions, remedy and the process for using it matter as much as the headline duration.

Does a batch code prove the cause of a defect?

No. It identifies material for investigation; causation and responsibility still require review.
