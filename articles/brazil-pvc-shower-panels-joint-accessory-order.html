PVC shower panels in Brazil: order joints and accessories

A quotation for the wall area alone does not describe a finished shower. A Brazilian distributor supplying bathroom renovations needs a repeatable accessory and documentation package, not simply a decorative panel and a promise that PVC is waterproof. This guide focuses on purchasing that package; it does not approve a particular panel for wet-area use.

Short answer

Confirm that the exact panel system is suitable for the proposed shower, then request its approved joint, corner and penetration details. Identify all required accessories and compatible sealing products. A water-resistant panel face does not establish the watertightness of a completed installation.

Turn the room drawing into a joint schedule

Mark internal corners, exposed ends, the shower-tray or bath interface and every pipe penetration. Separate the visible finish from the underlying waterproofing design, which must be reviewed by the responsible local professional. Record dimensions and photographs so suppliers can quote the actual interfaces. Do not let the installer discover an unpriced corner detail after the panels have reached the Brazilian site.

Request one documented system

Ask which trims, adhesives and sealants are approved together and what substrate conditions are required. Dumaplast’s instructions distinguish its panels from the installer’s responsibility for sealed joints. That distinction is useful for procurement, but its method does not certify other brands or replace local project requirements. Keep the current instructions for the selected system with the sample approval; a generic tube labelled silicone is not a compatibility statement.

Price accessories by the planned installation

List trim lengths, corners and penetrations separately from panel area. Ask the installer to estimate sealing-product quantities from the approved joint geometry and the product’s published coverage information. Include cutting allowances and agreed spare components without inventing a universal waste percentage. Identify which materials arrive with the import and which are bought locally, and obtain approval before accepting any proposed substitute.

Prepare a Portuguese acceptance package

Create a concise Portuguese checklist with original product codes and document revisions. Assign responsibility for substrate acceptance, concealed waterproofing, panel fitting and final inspection. Agree what the project professional will inspect before the shower is used; do not improvise a testing method from a marketing article. Retain photographs and maintenance instructions so a later repair can identify the correct trim or sealing product rather than relying on appearance alone.

Before you request a quotation

  • Dimensioned shower layout with corners and penetrations.
  • Written suitability and current system installation documents.
  • Accessory schedule and approved locally sourced substitutions.
  • Portuguese handover notes and named inspection responsibilities.

Frequently asked questions

Does waterproof PVC remove the need to check the wall behind it?

No. The substrate and the complete wet-area design still need assessment for the actual project.

Can an importer quote panels before all accessories are selected?

A preliminary quotation can identify exclusions, but the final order should resolve the interfaces needed to complete the installation.
