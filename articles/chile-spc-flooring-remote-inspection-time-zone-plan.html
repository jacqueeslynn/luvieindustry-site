SPC flooring for Chile: agree the remote inspection appointment before release

A Chilean buyer may arrange a video review of an SPC flooring order with a supplier, an inspector and a colleague working in another city. A message saying tomorrow morning is not a usable inspection appointment. If one decision maker joins late, the presenter may repeat only part of the review, leaving different participants with different evidence. Before arranging a remote session, agree the exact appointment and its purchasing purpose. This guide organizes attendance and records; it does not establish an inspection standard or claim that video can replace a qualified on-site inspection.

Short answer

Confirm the calendar date, participant locations and corresponding local times for that specific date. Name the people who must attend and distinguish observation from authority to release an order. Circulate the review scope and current documents before the call. If attendance or connectivity prevents a required check, record the gap and obtain an explicit next-step decision rather than treating the meeting as completed approval.

Use the inspection date, not a remembered time difference

Ask each participant which city or time zone they will actually be in during the session. Do not infer their location from a company address or country name. The Time and Date meeting-planner guidance explains that time differences can change during the year and that the selected date matters. Use a date-specific conversion and show each participant's local calendar date as well as the hour. Confirm any date rollover explicitly. Avoid asserting a permanent Chile-to-China offset or copying an invitation from an earlier season. A planning tool helps with conversion; it does not confirm that participants are available or that the supplier can present the order then.

Separate the appointment from the review scope

Send the SPC model, order reference and the exact questions to be reviewed before the call. State whether the session concerns carton identification, visible condition, document clarification or another agreed purpose. Have the responsible inspector or buyer define which checks require physical access, suitable instruments or independent sampling. Do not promise that a camera view proves dimensions, concealed construction or whole-order conformity. Identify the current document revision and where attendees can access it. Assign a host, a presenter and someone to record unresolved items so that the person moving the camera is not also expected to capture every purchasing decision.

Confirm required attendance and a practical fallback

List which participants are essential for each decision and who may observe without approving. Ask them to acknowledge the invitation with the local time displayed in their calendar, especially if the date changes. Check that the proposed platform and shared documents are accessible through their organizations' approved systems. Agree how to reconnect or reschedule if audio, video or access fails. A recording is not an automatic substitute for a required witness; discuss permission, access and retention before making one. If a reviewer is absent, do not silently transfer their authority to another attendee. Record which parts can proceed and which must wait for the authorized person.

Close the session with a traceable decision record

At the end, summarize what was actually shown, who attended and which questions remain open. Connect photographs or notes to the order and the relevant item rather than saving an unlabeled collection of screenshots. Ask the authorized buyer to distinguish acceptance for a limited purpose from shipment release, payment authorization or technical approval. Record any conditions and their owners. If another session is required, issue a new date-specific invitation and identify the outstanding checks; do not assume the previous time difference still applies. Keep the final decision in the agreed purchasing record, not solely in an informal chat reaction or a calendar attendance list.

Before you request a quotation

  • The date and local time are confirmed for every required participant.
  • The SPC reference, document revision and review questions are circulated.
  • Attendance requirements, authority and connection fallback are explicit.
  • Observed items, unresolved checks and release decisions are recorded separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does accepting the calendar invitation approve the order?

No. It confirms an attendance response, not product acceptance or release authority. The purchasing decision needs its own explicit record under the agreed process.

Can the team reuse last month's local times?

Do not assume so. Check the locations and the actual new date, then obtain attendance confirmation. A correct conversion still does not establish working-hour availability.
