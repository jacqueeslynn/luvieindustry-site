SPC flooring for Peru: reconcile packed weight before booking freight

An SPC flooring buyer in Peru may receive a quotation expressed by floor area, a packing proposal expressed by cartons and a freight request expressed by kilograms. Those records describe the same intended order from different viewpoints. A weight copied from an early sample or a different packing arrangement can therefore survive unnoticed until collection. Before releasing a booking, ask purchasing and the packing team to reconcile what is being weighed, which packaging is included and whether the number is measured or estimated. This guide proposes a buyer worksheet, not a product weight or transport limit.

Short answer

Keep product weight, packed-carton weight and complete pallet weight in separate fields with explicit units and scope. Connect each figure to the current quantity and packing revision. Ask the forwarder which values its booking requires, and resolve unexplained differences before treating a provisional total as final shipping information.

Define the boundary of each weight

Start with the offered SPC reference, planks per carton and carton count, using the supplier's current information rather than a previous order's spreadsheet. Label whether a figure covers the flooring alone, a closed carton or a loaded pallet including its packaging. Do not call every figure gross weight without identifying the object. Record any accessories or samples travelling with the order as separate items. An empty pallet is not part of the flooring's product weight, but it still travels with the shipment. The purpose of these distinctions is to make the information traceable; it does not establish the weight of any Luvie model or confirm a particular packing service is available.

Keep estimates visibly separate from measurements

For an early quotation, a supplier may only have an estimated packed weight. Mark it as an estimate, name its basis and identify who will replace it with the agreed final information. If representative cartons are weighed, record the selected carton references, packing condition, units and weighing record. Ask the responsible packing team to choose equipment and a method appropriate to the load. Do not infer a universal tolerance from the number of decimal places displayed. A table that combines estimates and measurements without labels looks more certain than its evidence. Agree which changes, such as a revised carton count or additional protection, require the total to be revisited.

Reconcile the carton and pallet totals

Build the total from identified packing groups rather than multiplying one convenient carton figure across unlike items. Check whether pallet, wrapping and protective materials have already been included before adding them again. Compare the resulting record with any complete-load measurement using the same scope and unit. If the totals differ, first investigate missing items, duplicated packaging, unit conversion and the packing revision. Do not force the spreadsheet to agree by changing a number without an explanation. Assign the discrepancy to a named reviewer and retain the correction history. A remaining difference needs an explicit resolution by the responsible parties, not a purchasing assumption that it is probably harmless.

Send the forwarder one controlled booking record

DHL's freight FAQ distinguishes actual gross weight, including packaging and pallets, from volumetric weight used in its chargeable-weight comparison. This is a useful reason not to substitute bare product weight for packed shipment information. It is not a Peru-specific tariff or a quotation for this order. Ask the selected forwarder to confirm the applicable booking fields and charging basis for the actual service. Keep dimensions and weights separately labelled, and do not calculate a shipping price from this guide. Issue the approved record with its date and packing revision; if packaging changes later, notify the forwarder and request confirmation of any booking implications before dispatch.

Before you request a quotation

  • Current product and packing references match the intended quantity.
  • Every weight has a unit, scope and estimate or measurement status.
  • Packaging is counted once and unexplained differences are resolved.
  • The forwarder receives the approved revision and any later changes.

Frequently asked questions

Can catalogue coverage establish shipping weight?

No. Coverage describes area. Request current weight and packing information for the offered model and actual order instead of converting area through an assumed weight factor.

Does a complete pallet weight replace all shipping weight declarations?

Not automatically. Ask the forwarder what the chosen service and shipment require. Keep the pallet record available without presenting it as a substitute for every transport document.
