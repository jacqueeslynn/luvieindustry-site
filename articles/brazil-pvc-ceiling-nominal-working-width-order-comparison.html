Brazil PVC ceiling orders: compare working width, not just the quoted board width

Two PVC ceiling quotations can show the same nominal width but different installed coverage. For a Brazilian distributor comparing profiles, the useful number is the width added by each connected board after its joint engages. This worked example is a calculation exercise, not a Luvie specification, available size or recommended waste allowance. Obtain the selected profile drawing and an assembled sample before turning it into an order.

Short answer

For an illustrative 4,000 mm wide ceiling, a 250 mm working width requires 16 rows. If 250 mm is instead the overall width and working coverage is 240 mm, the same span needs 17 rows. The difference comes from joint geometry and rounding, not a change in room area.

Measure the joint before comparing the price

Overall width may include a tongue or flange that overlaps its neighbour. Ask the supplier to mark overall and effective dimensions separately on the same cross-section. Assemble several boards, measure their combined installed coverage and check the result against the drawing. Do not decide the effective width from a photograph or subtract an assumed joint allowance from every supplier's product.

Round rows before calculating packs

Divide the ceiling span perpendicular to the boards by the confirmed working width, then round upward to a whole row. In the example, seventeen 240 mm rows provide 4,080 mm before the last row is trimmed. Sixteen provide only 3,840 mm. For a 3 m board length, the row calculation becomes sixteen or seventeen full-length boards only if that length actually suits the room and the perimeter detail. Length, direction and permissible joints are separate inputs.

A pack comparison can hide a real material difference

With the illustrative ten-board pack, both options require two packs for one room. That does not mean their coverage is identical: the unused board balance differs. Across ten identical rooms, sixteen rows require 160 boards and seventeen require 170, before any separately justified allowance. At ten boards per pack that is sixteen versus seventeen packs. Do not pool offcuts or spare boards across rooms unless the layout and finish batch permit it.

Put comparable units on the Brazilian quotation

Request the price per board or pack, the board length, effective coverage and pack count in the same quotation revision. Calculate material cost per installed square metre using working coverage, while retaining the actual pack purchase cost. Keep freight, taxes, trims and installation separate rather than presenting this coverage calculation as a landed-cost estimate. Ask whether the last-row fixing and minimum usable cut width change the layout.

Before you request a quotation

  • Obtain an exact model drawing with effective width identified.
  • Confirm board length, orientation and perimeter details.
  • Round rows and then pack quantities; keep each step visible.
  • Specify any damage or cutting allowance separately with a project basis.

Frequently asked questions

Does every 250 mm PVC ceiling board cover only 240 mm?

No. Both figures in this exercise are illustrative. Actual coverage depends on the selected profile and joint.

Can I use ceiling area divided by nominal board area?

It is only an initial estimate. Effective coverage, whole rows, board length, cuts and pack rounding determine the order.

Is the extra row a waste allowance?

No. It is required by the calculated span and working width. Damage and cutting allowances are separate.
