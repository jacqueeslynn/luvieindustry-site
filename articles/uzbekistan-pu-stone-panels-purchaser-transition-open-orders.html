Uzbekistan PU stone panels: hand over open orders when the purchasing contact changes

A new purchasing contact may inherit an inbox full of PU stone-panel photographs but no clear record of which texture was approved or what remains to be delivered. A similar-looking sample can then be mistaken for the ordered model, while unresolved finish questions disappear into an old chat. For an Uzbekistan importer or project buyer, the handover should preserve the identity and current decision status of each open order. This differs from transferring spare stock or moving a showroom display: the aim is to let a new buyer continue an unfinished transaction without silently changing its basis.

Short answer

Create an open-order handover that connects each purchase line to its confirmed model, texture reference, finish and approved sample. Reconcile ordered, delivered and outstanding quantities, list unresolved decisions with owners, and obtain acknowledgement from the incoming buyer and established supplier contact. A change of contact does not itself authorize a product substitution.

Recover the exact product identity

Start with the accepted purchase document rather than the latest attractive photograph. Record the PU model reference, stated dimensions and finish identification from that document. Where mould or texture references are available, include them explicitly and distinguish them from a commercial color name. Link the approved sample identifier and its acceptance record; a photograph without a reference may be useful context but is not enough to prove equivalence. If two records disagree, flag the conflict for confirmation before the incoming buyer places a repeat order. Do not invent missing model details from appearance. The handover should make uncertainty visible instead of making an incomplete file look complete.

Reconcile every open quantity

For each line, show the ordered quantity, documented receipts, any accepted changes and the balance still requiring action. Keep the units consistent and explain a conversion between panels and cartons using confirmed packing information. Separate a physically received quantity from one merely described as dispatched, and separate commercial acceptance from a transport update. List any held, rejected or disputed items without assuming the outcome of the claim. Link supporting records so the incoming buyer can reconstruct the balance. A spreadsheet total that cannot be traced to the source documents is not a reliable starting point for a replacement order or final delivery instruction.

Transfer decisions as well as documents

List pending sample approvals, finish clarifications, document requests and other open actions with the responsible person and the next required response. Include the latest agreed answer, not only the question that started the conversation. Identify who may approve changes under the buyer’s actual procedures; a newly copied colleague should not be treated as an authorized approver by assumption. ISO guidance recognizes the importance of documented information for operating and controlling processes. The handover proposed here is an application of that general idea, not a claim of certification or an Uzbekistan-specific legal requirement. Keep commercially sensitive records in the approved business system rather than attaching everything to a public inquiry.

Confirm the handover without changing the order

Arrange an acknowledgement that the incoming contact has received the current order basis and outstanding action list. Ask the established supplier contact to confirm the relevant commercial references and the correct route for subsequent questions. Keep any bank-detail change outside this routine product handover and subject to independent finance verification. If the incoming buyer wants a different texture or finish, initiate a separate documented change instead of editing the inherited reference. Retain the earlier correspondence as history while clearly identifying the current action owner. A successful handover ends with the next decision being obvious, not with an unsupported declaration that all open orders are complete.

Before you request a quotation

  • Tie each line to the accepted PU model and sample reference.
  • Reconcile delivered and outstanding quantities in confirmed units.
  • Assign an owner and next action to every unresolved decision.
  • Acknowledge the new contact without implying a product substitution.

Frequently asked questions

Can the new buyer approve a similar texture from a photograph?

A photograph can support discussion, but the proposed reference and the approval basis must be confirmed. Do not treat visual similarity as proof that the ordered model is unchanged.

Does a change of purchasing contact require a new order?

Not automatically. Follow the actual contract and internal procedures. Preserve the existing order basis, then document any genuine scope change separately.
