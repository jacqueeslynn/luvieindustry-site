SPC flooring storage racks for Uzbekistan showrooms

SPC flooring storage racks in Uzbekistan should be chosen around the delivered cartons, not a photograph of a tidy warehouse. A retailer may need to hold saleable stock behind the showroom while allowing staff to retrieve individual cartons. The storage arrangement must preserve product condition and safe access without encouraging unsupported stacks or improvised lifting.

Short answer

Obtain current carton dimensions, gross weight and storage instructions before choosing a rack. Have the rack supplier confirm the intended load arrangement. Keep display samples separate from saleable cartons and never infer rack capacity from shelf size.

Independent reference

HSE construction manual handling guidance. General guidance, not a Luvie product certification.

Start with the carton rather than the plank

Request external carton dimensions, cartons per pallet, gross weights and the manufacturer’s storage orientation. A plank specification does not describe the shipping package. Check whether several lengths or packing formats are planned for the assortment. Map the largest and heaviest current carton, but also define how future packing changes will be reviewed. The warehouse record should identify which measurements are confirmed and which are still provisional.

Have the rack arrangement assessed

Provide the rack supplier with carton placement, loading pattern and how stock will be retrieved. Do not convert a shelf’s advertised capacity into a permission to concentrate the full load on a small area. Check deck support, clearances and access with the responsible supplier or competent person. HSE manual-handling guidance offers general task-assessment principles; it is not an Uzbekistan rack design standard and supplies no product-specific capacity for your installation.

Plan retrieval without disturbing neighbouring stock

A customer requesting one carton should not force staff to drag it from beneath an unstable pile. Define picking locations, replenishment access and where opened cartons may be inspected. Keep walkways clear and avoid relying on a ladder while carrying a bulky load. If mechanical handling equipment is planned, verify the actual aisles, turning space and floor conditions before the racks are filled. Staff should know where damaged packaging is isolated.

Keep environmental and product instructions visible

Storage requirements belong with the selected flooring documentation. Avoid assuming that a retail room is automatically suitable because it is indoors. Ask how to protect cartons from local water sources, direct sunlight and construction activity, and follow the supplier’s stated conditions. Quick-Step’s installation resources are an example of why flooring instructions are model-specific; do not copy another manufacturer’s temperature or stacking values into a Luvie order.

Reconcile physical stock and sample stock

Put the model and batch location in the inventory record, with opened cartons clearly distinguished from intact saleable packs. A display plank removed for a demonstration should not silently reduce the coverage advertised for a complete carton. Record count adjustments when a carton is split. Before a new delivery, check whether the planned bay still fits the confirmed package and whether a previous product has occupied its space.

Before you request a quotation

  • Confirm current external carton dimensions and gross weight.
  • Ask the rack supplier to approve the loading arrangement.
  • Define safe picking and replenishment access.
  • Separate intact cartons, opened stock and display pieces.

Frequently asked questions

Can cartons be stored upright to save space?

Use the selected product’s storage instructions. Do not assume upright storage is acceptable merely because it fits the showroom layout.

Does a heavy-duty shelf label settle the question?

No. Capacity depends on the assessed loading arrangement and rack configuration, not just the words heavy duty.
