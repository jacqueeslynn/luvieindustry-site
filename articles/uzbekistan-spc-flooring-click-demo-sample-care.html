Keep SPC click demonstrations honest and usable

SPC click demonstration samples in Uzbekistan help buyers understand how a flooring joint is assembled, but a repeatedly handled showroom piece is not the same as a newly supplied plank. Forcing a worn sample together can teach the wrong method and create an unfair impression of the product. A dealer needs a controlled demonstration routine and clear limits on what it proves.

Short answer

Use the selected model’s installation method, keep demonstration pieces separate from appearance references and retire damaged joints. A successful tabletop connection does not establish whole-floor performance, subfloor suitability or unlimited reuse.

Identify the exact joint system

Ask for the current installation instructions for the selected SPC model and confirm that the demonstration pieces come from it. Similar-looking edge profiles can require different assembly sequences. Quick-Step’s installation resources illustrate why manufacturer-specific instructions matter; they must not be copied as Luvie instructions. Keep the correct document beside the sample so sales staff are not relying on a method remembered from a previous collection.

Give the demonstration a stable setup

Use a suitable support surface and enough space to show the instructed movement without twisting or forcing the pieces. Keep the exposed edges visible to the viewer while protecting them between demonstrations. Do not add tools, adhesive or tapping methods that the selected instructions do not allow. If the staff cannot demonstrate the joint confidently, request clarification instead of improvising in front of the customer.

Inspect before every repeated use

Look for chipped edges, contamination or deformation that could affect the demonstration. Mark the kit as a demonstration set rather than returning used pieces to saleable cartons. Ask the supplier whether repeated disassembly is permitted and what limitations apply. Do not promise that a floor can be taken apart and rebuilt indefinitely because a small sample has survived several demonstrations. The sample’s history should be clear to staff.

Explain the boundary of the exercise

Tell the buyer that the demonstration shows the joint and intended assembly concept. It does not prove suitability for a particular subfloor, room layout, load or operating condition. A tabletop assembly cannot replace project review and installation documentation. Invite practical questions, but separate what the demonstration visibly shows from what requires a technical answer. This builds credibility more effectively than an unsupported claim of universal easy installation.

Record recurring questions for the quote

After the demonstration, capture the buyer’s actual concern: installer training, a transition detail, replacement access or project conditions. Send those questions with the model reference when requesting a quotation. If the joint design changes, replace the kit and update the demonstration instructions together. Keep the old version out of active circulation so customers do not approve a connection that is no longer supplied.

Before you request a quotation

  • Use the exact current model and instructions.
  • Separate demo pieces from saleable stock.
  • Inspect joints and retire damaged samples.
  • Explain what the demonstration does not prove.

Frequently asked questions

Can the same sample be clicked together indefinitely?

Do not assume unlimited reuse. Follow the selected product’s guidance and retire pieces whose condition no longer represents a new joint.

Does an easy demonstration guarantee an easy project installation?

No. The actual substrate, layout, site conditions and installer method still matter and require their own review.
