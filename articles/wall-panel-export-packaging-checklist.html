Wall Panel Packaging Checklist for Overseas Buyers

An export packaging checklist covering cartons, labels, protection, loading, documentation, and receiving inspection.

Direct answer

Wall panel packaging should protect the product, identify every SKU and support efficient receiving. Confirm carton construction, quantity, internal protection, labels, pallet or container method, loading photos and the receiving inspection plan before shipment.

Design packaging around the product

Long profiles, glossy boards, deep textures and flooring cartons face different damage risks. Review edge, corner, surface and moisture protection for the selected products instead of applying one generic packing statement.

Make every carton traceable

Labels should match the purchase order and identify model, color, quantity and any private-label requirement. Consistent coding reduces warehouse mistakes and makes claims easier to investigate.

Connect loading to receiving

Agree loading sequence, carton count and photo records. The buyer should inspect container condition, count and visible damage promptly on arrival and retain evidence before distribution.

Buyer checklist

  • Carton material and product quantity
  • Edge, corner and surface protection
  • Model, color and buyer label
  • Pallet, bundle or loose-loading method
  • Loading record and arrival inspection

Questions buyers ask

Is stronger packaging always better?

Packaging must balance protection, weight, cost, handling and local requirements.

Can Luvie use buyer branding?

Private-label packaging is supported; artwork, language, marks and quantities are confirmed per order.

What evidence helps with a damage claim?

Keep loading photos, shipping documents and immediate arrival photos showing container and affected cartons.
