UV marble sheets in Colombia: plan unloading and internal handling

Large decorative sheets can arrive undamaged yet become difficult to move through the building. Colombian project buyers should check the route from delivery vehicle to installation area before selecting sheet sizes and agreeing dispatch.

Short answer

Confirm packed and individual-sheet dimensions and weights, then have the site team assess access, equipment and storage. Use a task-specific handling plan; do not select a lifting method from an illustration or assume a fixed number of people is sufficient.

Survey the complete delivery route

Record the unloading location, doorway openings, turns, lifts, stairs and final staging area. Include any time restrictions imposed by the building. Compare the route with the packed size as well as the bare sheet size. A sheet may fit a wall but not the available lift or turning space, so resolve the route before purchase rather than after arrival.

Ask for information the handling team can use

Request dimensions, weight, packaging type and any supplier handling restrictions. SafeWork SA’s sheet-material guidance emphasizes advance access planning and suitable mechanical assistance. It is a safety reference, not Colombian legal advice or a handling approval for a specific Luvie sheet. Have the local site professional select the method and equipment for the actual task and conditions.

Prepare a protected staging area

Agree where unopened packs and individual sheets will wait, keeping the route clear. Confirm support, surface protection and environmental requirements with the product supplier. Avoid leaning or stacking material according to convenience alone. Plan the installation sequence to reduce repeated moves and keep packaging information available until the receiving and condition checks are complete.

Connect delivery acceptance with the work plan

For the Colombian order, name the person who receives the shipment and the person who authorizes internal movement. Record condition before unpacking and after any necessary repacking. If access changes, stop and revise the plan rather than cutting material simply to fit a doorway. Include handling and staging responsibilities in the quotation comparison so they are not assumed to be free.

Before you request a quotation

  • Packed and individual dimensions and weights.
  • Measured access route and building delivery restrictions.
  • Site-approved equipment, method and responsible personnel.
  • Protected staging area and condition-recording process.

Frequently asked questions

Can two people always carry a decorative sheet safely?

No universal team size is appropriate. Assess the sheet, route, task and equipment with the responsible site professional.

Should I remove all packaging at the delivery point?

Only as allowed by the receiving and handling plan. Protection and identification may still be needed for internal movement.
