SPC flooring for Colombian offices: check chair and rolling-load use

Office procurement should begin with how the floor will be used, not only how it looks in a sample photograph. Wheeled chairs and repeated trolley routes deserve specific questions when comparing SPC proposals for Colombia.

Short answer

Describe the chairs, wheel types and repeated travel paths to the supplier, and request explicit suitability and care guidance. A commercial label or static-load figure alone does not establish suitability for repeated rolling use.

Map the actual work patterns

Separate desk areas, meeting rooms, reception and storage routes. Record where chairs move continuously and where loaded equipment travels. Include wheel material and contact details when known, without guessing loads. This gives the supplier a useful application brief and helps the project team consider a different flooring system where a rigid-core option cannot be supported.

Ask a narrower question than “commercial grade”

Request the document that addresses the actual use, including limitations and required protection. Karndean’s office guidance discourages its rigid-core luxury vinyl in areas with caster-wheel furniture and encourages chair mats for vinyl flooring. This is that manufacturer’s advice, not a blanket rule for all SPC. It demonstrates why buyers need an explicit answer for the selected product rather than a generic marketing label.

Review protection as part of the specification

If a protection mat is proposed, confirm its compatibility with both the flooring and the chair. Check edges, stability, cleaning access and whether it introduces an obstruction. Ask for a maintenance routine that addresses debris under wheels and mats. Do not claim that a mat makes an otherwise unsuitable product acceptable without the supplier’s written agreement.

Align the quotation with the office handover

For a Colombian office order, identify who supplies chairs and floor protection so the responsibilities are not split invisibly between vendors. Include the agreed use limitations in the Spanish handover notes. Keep approved sample and product identifiers with the warranty documents. If seating changes after approval, review the floor specification again before purchasing.

Before you request a quotation

  • Office zones and frequency of wheeled movement.
  • Chair and trolley wheel details where available.
  • Product-specific suitability and protection requirements.
  • Responsibility for mats, cleaning and handover instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Does a thick wear layer prove resistance to rolling chairs?

No. Ask for evidence and guidance relevant to that use and the complete product construction.

Should the chair supplier be involved?

Yes. Flooring, wheel type and any protective mat should be reviewed together by the relevant suppliers and project team.
