PU stone panels in the UAE: transfer the finish register when the operator changes

A new facilities team can inherit attractive PU stone-effect walls without knowing which decorative reference belongs to each elevation. Purchasing then receives a photograph and a request for something similar, while the original approval files remain in an outgoing contractor's mailbox. For a UAE interior project, make the finish-location register part of the operational handover. This guide concerns information continuity before a future purchasing decision; it does not prescribe installation details, establish local legal duties or confirm that a particular Luvie model suits the building.

Short answer

Transfer a controlled register that connects each installed wall to an identifiable product reference, available approval records and a responsible contact. Check it together on site before accepting it as complete. Distinguish documented facts from unresolved identifications, and keep later repair changes visible rather than treating the original order as a permanent record of every surface.

Start with locations that the incoming team can find

Give each relevant wall a stable location description: building, floor, room and elevation, using the project's existing naming convention where possible. Include an annotated overview photograph or drawing reference rather than relying on a close-up of the stone texture. A close-up helps compare appearance but rarely proves which wall it represents. Walk representative areas with the incoming team and check that their staff can find the recorded locations without the outgoing supervisor's memory. Note inaccessible areas and assign someone to resolve them. Do not invent reference numbers for unidentified material merely to make the register appear complete.

Connect product identity to the evidence actually held

Record the supplier, offered model or finish reference and relevant purchase or approval identifiers where those records exist. Separate an approved sample reference from an invoice description: they answer different questions and may not contain the same level of detail. Link the current care information and any project-specific restrictions approved by the responsible professionals. AIA Contract Documents discusses maintenance documentation and spare inventories as handover considerations; that supports the general value of an organized transfer, not a UAE compliance conclusion. The location register proposed here is a practical purchasing tool. It does not prove fire performance, adhesive compatibility or suitability merely because a catalogue is attached.

Reconcile repairs before the old team leaves

Ask whether any areas have been replaced, repainted or otherwise altered since the original installation. Mark those locations and attach the available change record. Where a repair's product identity cannot be verified, record the uncertainty explicitly and route it to the designated reviewer. Avoid copying the original finish code across the entire room if a later intervention changed only one wall. Keep historical records available, but identify the version that describes the current known condition. Photographs should carry a location and date so that an older pre-repair image cannot quietly become the basis for a new quotation.

Test the transfer with a realistic purchasing question

Ask the incoming team to select one wall and retrieve its reference, supporting record and escalation contact using only the transferred package. This simple retrieval exercise exposes broken links, restricted folders and ambiguous labels before the outgoing account is closed. Agree where the controlled master will live and who can update it after future work. Transfer necessary access through the organization's approved process; do not share personal passwords or leave customer information in public folders. Record what was received, what remains unresolved and who owns each outstanding item. Acceptance of the information package should not be described as technical approval of the installed assembly.

Before you request a quotation

  • Wall locations can be found from the register without relying on personal memory.
  • Product references link to available purchase, approval and care records.
  • Repairs and uncertain identifications are visible and assigned for review.
  • The incoming operator can retrieve records and knows who maintains the master.

Frequently asked questions

Can a photograph identify the exact replacement finish?

Not reliably on its own. Lighting, surface changes and similar patterns can mislead. Use the documented reference and request a current sample for the appropriate reviewer before committing to replacement material.

Does transferring the register guarantee future availability?

No. A historical record helps describe the request; it does not guarantee stock, continuity of finish or equivalence of a later supply. Ask the supplier to confirm the current offer separately.
