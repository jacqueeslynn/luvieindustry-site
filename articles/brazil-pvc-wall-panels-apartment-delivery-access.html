PVC wall panels for Brazilian apartments: will the ordered length reach the room?

PVC wall panels may fit the room drawing but not the route from the truck to that room. For a Brazilian distributor supplying apartment renovations, a long package creates a different planning problem from a small sample. Before confirming panel length, ask the receiving team to check the complete route and the building’s delivery arrangements. This guide is a purchasing checklist, not permission to exceed lift limits or improvise handling.

Short answer

Approve the packaged dimensions and weight against the whole delivery route, not just the elevator cabin. Resolve turns, door openings, handling space and storage before the order is released. A shorter option is useful only if its joints and installed appearance are also approved.

Request package dimensions, not only panel dimensions

Ask for the proposed bundle length, width, depth and gross weight, including protective packaging and any pallet. Record whether delivery is palletized or in individually handled bundles. Confirm which dimensions are estimates and obtain an updated packing proposal before dispatch. A product dimension does not describe the space needed for a trolley, handling equipment or a turn. Keep these details with the purchase order so the local carrier receives the same information.

Check the route with the building representative

For a São Paulo or another Brazilian apartment delivery, ask the building representative about the loading point, service entrance, lift rules and booking arrangements for that specific address. Measure clear door openings and corridor turns with the receiving contractor. Include access to the final room, not only the lobby. Have authorized personnel assess permitted lift loads and safe handling; do not assume a package fits because its length is below a cabin diagonal.

Compare alternatives before cutting becomes the fallback

If access fails, ask the supplier whether another available length or panel system meets the design. Compare the extra joints, trim requirements, usable coverage and waste, not merely the shorter package. Do not assume factory cutting is available or that site cutting preserves the approved joint geometry. A route change, different handling arrangement or split delivery needs written agreement with the relevant parties. Never plan to bend a rigid panel to force it through a doorway.

Agree storage and the delivery boundary

Identify a protected receiving area with the support conditions required by the selected manufacturer. The linked Dumaplast instructions specify flat transport for its own XL panels; use this as a reason to request product-specific handling guidance, not as a universal Luvie specification. State who unloads, who moves material upstairs and where the carrier’s service ends. Arrange an inspection point before packages are distributed to rooms, and record visible damage or missing bundles according to the agreed receiving process.

Before you request a quotation

  • Packed dimensions and gross weight
  • Site-checked route and building arrangements
  • Approved alternative if the route fails
  • Storage location and unloading responsibilities

Frequently asked questions

Does delivery to the address include carrying panels upstairs?

Not necessarily. Obtain the carrier’s written service scope and arrange any additional handling separately.

Can we order shorter panels and keep the same layout?

Only after reviewing the resulting joints, trims, quantity and approved installation details.
