PVC wall panels for Saudi buyers: hand over purchasing approvals before an absence

A wall-panel order can stall when the person who knows its history is away. More seriously, a substitute buyer may see a recent quotation and assume that the colour, construction and commercial scope have all been approved. For Saudi distributors and project purchasing teams, an absence handover should preserve the difference between information received and permission to act. This guide proposes an internal working method; it does not establish Saudi legal requirements, signatory authority or any Luvie supply terms.

Short answer

Prepare a dated decision register that identifies the selected product, the evidence supporting each approval, unresolved questions and the person authorized to make the next decision. Give the substitute buyer explicit limits and a reliable escalation contact. Forwarding an email chain is not the same as handing over authority to accept a substitution, approve a sample or place an order.

Describe the current decision, not just the latest attachment

Start with the exact PVC product reference and the project or distributor account concerned. List the current quotation revision, sample identifier and relevant drawings, then label each as proposed, under review or approved. A document received yesterday may be less authoritative than an older signed decision, so distinguish document date from approval status. Summarize why any alternative was rejected, without treating an informal preference as a technical conclusion. Include a location for the original evidence instead of copying fragments into several spreadsheets. The substitute should be able to identify what can proceed and what must remain on hold without interpreting weeks of messages.

Separate sample, technical and commercial authority

Name the person responsible for visual sample acceptance, the project professional who resolves technical suitability and the person authorized internally to commit the purchase. One colleague may hold several roles, but do not assume that approving a colour authorizes a different product construction or purchase quantity. Define which decisions the substitute can make and which require escalation. If a proposed change affects more than one area, identify the approvals that must be revisited together. ISO quality-management information is useful background for documented responsibilities, not evidence that this procedure is required by a particular clause or that Luvie is certified. Local company authority and contract requirements remain for the responsible organization to determine.

Give unresolved questions a stop condition and an owner

Turn vague notes such as awaiting confirmation into explicit questions: which product reference needs clarification, what evidence is requested, who should answer and what action depends on that answer? Do not invent a response deadline or supplier commitment. Record the agreed follow-up date separately from any promised delivery date. If the supplier offers an alternative during the absence, ask the substitute to capture its identity and differences rather than treating availability as approval. Keep technical suitability, sample appearance and commercial terms open until their respective owners respond. A missing answer should remain visible in the register, not disappear because a new quotation has arrived or the project team wants a quick update.

Close the handover with a reconciliation

Before the original buyer leaves, review one realistic scenario with the substitute: a changed reference, a revised quantity or an unanswered sample question. Confirm where the substitute records a decision and how the responsible people are notified. When the original buyer returns, compare the decision register with actual orders, supplier acknowledgements and sample records. Resolve discrepancies before issuing additional instructions that could contradict work already authorized. Retain superseded documents as history while clearly identifying the current version. This is an internal coordination safeguard, not a guarantee against delays or mistakes. It also does not authorize a substitute to make bank-detail changes or send payment without the organization's separate verification controls.

Before you request a quotation

  • Current product, quotation and sample references identified with status.
  • Visual, technical and commercial decision owners named.
  • Open questions linked to hold points and escalation contacts.
  • Substitute's limits and return-to-work reconciliation recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Does the newest quotation replace a previous approval?

Not automatically. Compare its reference and scope with the approved decision, and ask the appropriate owner to review changes before acting.

Can the substitute approve a different colour to save time?

Only within the authority actually delegated by the organization and after any required sample review. Urgency alone is not approval.
