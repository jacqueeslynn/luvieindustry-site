PU stone panels in Uzbekistan: agree repair materials before handover

A small edge chip can become a visible problem when maintenance staff do not know which finish system was used. Uzbekistan buyers should ask about documented repair options while the approved PU stone-panel sample and supplier contacts are still available.

Short answer

Ask the supplier which damage, if any, can be repaired with an approved method and which requires replacement. Test any proposed repair on an offcut first. Do not assume generic filler or paint will match the panel’s texture, coating or intended use.

Separate a cosmetic mark from a functional problem

Record the location, size and nature of the damage before choosing a remedy. Loose fixing, extensive cracking or an underlying wall problem should not be hidden with a surface touch-up. Ask the installer or responsible professional to review the cause. Define which minor cosmetic repairs the supplier supports and keep unsupported repair ideas out of the maintenance instructions.

Request a complete repair method

Ask for compatible materials, preparation, curing and finish guidance, including safety documents where relevant. Orac’s FAQ provides examples of product-specific joint and finishing instructions for its decorative products. Those materials are not automatically suitable for Luvie PU panels. Confirm the exact selected panel and coating before accepting any repair recommendation.

Approve the result on a spare piece

Use a representative offcut to assess texture, colour and sheen after the proposed repair has cured as instructed. Review it under the lighting and viewing distance relevant to the project. Photograph the result with the sample identifier and note any limits. If the finish cannot be matched acceptably, plan replacement rather than promising an invisible repair.

Make the handover useful months later

For a project in Uzbekistan, retain panel identifiers, approved repair instructions and the location of any spare pieces. Label maintenance materials and record their storage and shelf-life requirements. Agree who may carry out work and who should be contacted if damage exceeds the approved scope. A small organized handover file is more useful than an unlabelled container left on site.

Before you request a quotation

  • Selected panel and coating identifiers.
  • Supported repair scope and replacement criteria.
  • Approved offcut trial and compatible material documents.
  • Labelled spares, storage instructions and maintenance contact.

Frequently asked questions

Can ordinary wall filler repair every PU panel?

Do not assume compatibility or an acceptable finish. Obtain a product-specific method and test it first.

Should every chip be repaired instead of replaced?

No. Decide from the damage, approved method and acceptable result; some cases need replacement or further investigation.
