SPC flooring in Uzbekistan: underfloor heating approval

An SPC sample can establish colour and texture, but it cannot establish compatibility with a heated floor. For an apartment or showroom project in Uzbekistan, resolve the heating proposal before committing to a flooring shipment. The useful question is not whether SPC works with heating in general, but whether the exact combination is documented.

Short answer

Request written compatibility confirmation for the selected SPC construction, backing, heating system and substrate. Obtain the applicable operating limits and commissioning instructions from the responsible suppliers. Do not transfer another manufacturer’s limits to the product you are buying.

Build one brief for both suppliers

Give the flooring supplier and heating contractor the same drawing. Identify whether the system is water-based or electric, how it is incorporated into the substrate, and which rooms are controlled separately. Include the proposed attached backing or additional underlay. Ask each party to identify missing information rather than accepting two unrelated statements that their individual product is suitable. Keep the actual model identifiers on the brief.

Ask about the complete assembly

Request thermal-resistance information where available and ask the heating designer to assess the complete floor build-up. The referenced Karndean guidance illustrates product-specific controls and compatibility checks; it is not approval for Luvie flooring. Distinguish room air temperature, thermostat setting and the temperature at the flooring interface. Avoid copying a numerical limit into your purchase specification without confirming where it is measured and which product it covers.

Agree the site sequence before delivery

Have the installer document substrate acceptance, heating-system commissioning, the installation environment and the permitted restart procedure. These are responsibilities to settle, not generic instructions that a seller can infer from a country name. For a Tashkent project, for example, identify who will keep the approved records in the language used by the local installation team. Confirm that translated notes retain the original units and qualifications.

Include the occupied-room layout

Show rugs, fixed furniture and other areas where heat can be trapped, then ask the project professionals to assess the layout. Record the approved operation and maintenance instructions in the handover package. Compare quotations only after the scope is aligned: boards, permitted backing, accessories and technical documentation. If compatibility remains unconfirmed, hold the flooring selection rather than treating the shipment deadline as technical approval.

Before you request a quotation

  • Exact SPC and backing identifiers, not only colour and thickness.
  • Heating system model, layout and proposed substrate assembly.
  • Written supplier responses and installer commissioning responsibilities.
  • Destination city, approximate quantity and unresolved approval questions.

Frequently asked questions

Can the thermostat setting prove the floor is within its limit?

Not on its own. Ask the heating designer how the relevant temperature is measured and controlled for the approved assembly.

Should I order an extra insulating underlay?

Only if the flooring and heating suppliers approve it. Additional layers can change both mechanical behaviour and heat transfer.
