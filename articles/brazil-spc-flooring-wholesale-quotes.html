SPC flooring for Brazil: how to compare wholesale quotes

Two SPC flooring quotations can look almost identical until the first shipment reaches your Brazilian warehouse. One includes backing and export packing; the other leaves both open. A useful comparison starts with a common buying brief, not the lowest headline price. This guide helps importers turn different offers into a decision they can explain to their purchasing team.

Short answer

Compare the same product construction and delivery scope first. Then add shipment-specific freight and locally verified destination costs. Keep estimated costs separate from confirmed supplier prices.

Give every supplier the same specification

State plank dimensions, total thickness, whether that thickness includes backing, wear layer, surface finish, locking system and intended use. Ask suppliers to identify deviations instead of silently quoting their nearest alternative. A thicker plank is not automatically a better purchase if its construction or evidence does not meet the project brief. Request samples of the quoted reference and retain the approved one. For a Brazilian distributor, a Portuguese sales description should preserve the original product code so showroom staff and the overseas supplier discuss the same item.

Compare packing and loading, not only price per square metre

Ask for pieces per carton, coverage per carton, carton dimensions, gross weight and the proposed shipment quantity. Distinguish ordered area from installed area: cutting losses and project reserves need their own plan. If one offer uses pallets and another does not, ask the forwarder about handling and loading implications before treating the prices as equivalent. The named delivery place and trade terms should appear on both offers. A port quote is not necessarily a warehouse-delivered price.

Build a Brazil-specific cost worksheet

Brazil’s official import simulator provides a route to checking tax and administrative treatment by classification. Have the importer or customs adviser confirm the applicable classification and current requirements for the actual goods; do not copy a generic SPC tax percentage from a competitor’s blog. Add current freight, destination handling, clearance and inland delivery to the comparison. Record destination state, currency, exchange-rate assumption and quote expiry. Keep estimated cash outlay separate from any tax-credit treatment that your accountant must assess. This is a procurement worksheet, not a tax ruling.

Choose the offer you can verify and repeat

Separate firm costs, provisional costs and missing information. Ask whether the finish and construction can be reordered under the same reference, and how changes will be communicated. Agree payment milestones, inspection scope and a documented process for discrepancies without assuming any standard warranty. A slightly lower unit price can lose its advantage when cartons contain less coverage or the shipment excludes services you expected. Do not sign off until the comparison uses one consistent quantity basis and each exception has an owner.

Before you request a quotation

  • Exact construction and approved sample reference
  • Carton coverage, dimensions and gross weight
  • Named delivery place, currency and validity
  • Destination cost assumptions reviewed locally

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose the lowest FOB price?

Only after matching specifications and calculating the remaining delivery costs. FOB alone is not a warehouse cost.

Can a blog provide the final Brazilian tax rate?

No. Classification, operation details and current rules need a qualified local check.
