PU stone-look panels or gypsum relief for an Uzbekistan feature wall?

An Uzbekistan showroom can achieve a textured feature wall with different materials, but a PU stone-look panel and a cast gypsum decoration should not be compared as if they were the same item. Their quoted finish stages, joint work and handling plans may differ. Ask what arrives at the site and what work remains before the wall is ready for customers.

Short answer

Compare the exact finished systems: declared weight, substrate and fixing requirements, joint treatment, painting or touch-up work, and replacement logistics. Do not assume all gypsum products are identical or that every PU panel arrives fully finished. Approve a representative sample of each proposal before comparing complete delivered and installed costs.

Independent reference

Define the gypsum product and the PU finish

Ask whether the gypsum proposal is a cast decorative panel, a reinforced architectural element or another construction; ordinary gypsum board is a different comparison. USG’s industrial catalog lists distinct casting and architectural-ornamentation products, illustrating why the generic word “gypsum” is insufficient. Its data do not certify a local fabricator’s finished panel. For the PU offer, identify the chosen relief, surface coating and any site finishing still required. Put those details next to the model reference on the quotation.

Use declared weights and approved support details

Request the weight and dimensions of each actual item and the proposed complete wall build-up. Do not estimate weight from a photograph or apply a generic material-density figure to an unknown panel. Have the installer or responsible professional review the existing wall and support proposal. Discuss handling, access and protection of raised details with the delivery team. A small sample being easy to lift does not establish the suitability of a large installation or its fixing method.

Include jointing, painting and the approval sample

Ask which joint materials, primers, coatings or repair materials are included, and who supplies and applies them. If the gypsum finish is painted on site while the PU option has a supplied decorative surface, compare both at the same completed stage. Review a joint, an edge and a representative relief under the intended lighting. Record the accepted appearance and any remaining trial work. For a Tashkent shop opening, identify site finishing as a separate scheduling dependency rather than inventing a drying time or promising that either option needs no finishing.

Compare delivery risk and future repair on equal terms

Ask for actual packing dimensions, protection details and a receiving procedure for both offers. Separate material cost, freight, handling, installation and final decoration. Keep damage reporting and responsibility clear without assuming that one material never chips or that the other always survives transport. Request an approved repair approach and identify spare pieces or finishes by model and batch where available. Choose against the project’s appearance, evidence and maintenance requirements, not an unsupported claim that one category is always cheaper or stronger.

Before you request a quotation

  • Uzbekistan destination, wall dimensions and clearly defined material proposals.
  • Declared item weights, packing information and reviewed support requirements.
  • Jointed appearance samples and complete site-finishing responsibilities.
  • Comparable delivered/installed quotations and documented repair references.

Frequently asked questions

Are all decorative gypsum panels equivalent?

No. Ask for the exact product, construction and finished-panel data. A raw-material catalog does not describe every fabricated decoration.

Are PU stone-look panels always supplied ready to install without finishing?

Do not assume that. Confirm the selected model’s supplied finish, joint treatment and any required site touch-up work.
