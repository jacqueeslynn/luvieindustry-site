UAE: compare SPC click and glue-down LVT for phased retail renovation

Ask both bidders to price the same opening schedule, not just the same floor area. A receiving bay, a sales aisle and a shop entrance may have different rolling loads and different periods when installers can take possession. Give both suppliers the same zone map and ask them to identify exceptions to the installation instructions for the exact product quoted.

Procurement checklist

For a floating click system, ask the installer to mark where movement breaks and transitions would go. For glue-down LVT, ask which adhesive and substrate preparation the proposed system needs, and when each zone can reopen under the stated site conditions. Do not substitute the plank supplier's general guidance for the adhesive instructions.

Compare the bids by zone: installed material, substrate preparation, transitions, temporary protection and time out of service. Request a trial at an entrance that includes both a door and a transition. Inspect the finished height and ask how an isolated damaged plank would be replaced after the store opens. Choose the system that can be installed and serviced under your actual access constraints.
