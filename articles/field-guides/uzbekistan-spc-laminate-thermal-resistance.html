Uzbekistan: compare SPC and laminate thermal resistance

For a heated apartment or showroom in Uzbekistan, “suitable for underfloor heating” is only the beginning of a flooring comparison. A buyer choosing SPC or laminate needs the thermal resistance of the complete proposed build-up, not just plank thickness. Two visually similar floors can place different resistance between the heating system and the room. This guide uses named manufacturer examples to show how to read the figures; they are not specifications for Luvie products.

Quick answer

Compare R-values in m²K/W for the exact floor plus its approved underlay. Lower resistance means less opposition to heat transfer through that layer, but it does not by itself establish running cost, heating capacity or installation approval. Ask the heating designer to check the complete assembly.

What the number measures

Thermal resistance expresses how strongly a layer opposes heat flow. Keep it separate from thermal conductivity, expressed in W/(m·K), and from total thickness in millimetres. An attached pad belongs in the build-up; adding another underlay changes the assembly and may not be permitted. Do not add an underlay value again if a published figure already includes it.

Why the underlay can change the shortlist

The two 8 mm laminate examples differ by 0.133 m²K/W even though the board thickness is unchanged. That comparison illustrates a purchasing mistake: selecting a plank first, then accepting whichever underlay is locally available. Record the underlay name and revision in the quotation before comparing systems.

In these named examples, the laminate assembly at 0.065 sits inside the stated Alpha Vinyl range. It would therefore be misleading to claim that every vinyl floor has lower resistance than every laminate floor. Neither figure demonstrates a particular reduction in an apartment’s energy bill. Room heat loss, controls and operating temperatures are separate design inputs.

A practical comparison sheet for a Tashkent project

Create one row per proposed assembly. Include the floor SKU, attached pad, separate underlay, each stated R-value, whether the value is measured or calculated, and the source document. Ask the supplier to mark clearly which layers the reported total covers. Leave unsupported cells blank rather than substituting a typical internet value.

Next ask the heating designer to confirm the required output with that assembly. Record the permitted surface temperature, commissioning procedure and any restrictions concerning rugs, fixed furniture or different heating zones from the exact installation instructions. A low R-value does not override these conditions.

Before issuing the purchase order, send the installer the same assembly sheet used for bid evaluation. If a substitute underlay is proposed later, reopen the thermal review instead of treating it as an equivalent accessory. The approved combination, not the material family name, should survive from quotation to delivery.
