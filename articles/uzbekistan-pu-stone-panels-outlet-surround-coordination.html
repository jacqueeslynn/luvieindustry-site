PU stone panels for Uzbekistan: coordinate outlet surrounds before ordering

An electrical outlet interrupts the relief of a stone-effect wall in a way that a plain colour sample cannot show. For an Uzbekistan showroom or hotel interior, the buyer needs to coordinate the decorative finish with the actual outlet cover before the panel order is released. This guide addresses purchasing and appearance coordination, not electrical installation. Electrical design, alterations and safety acceptance belong with a qualified local electrician.

Short answer

Identify each outlet and switch on the wall elevation, record the selected cover and proposed surround, and approve a representative non-energized mockup. Keep appearance approval separate from the electrician's technical acceptance. Ask for the exact Luvie panel instructions and accessory availability; do not copy another manufacturer's outlet detail or assume that a decorative surround is an electrical component.

Map the interruption before calculating finished wall coverage

Give each outlet or switch a reference on the current wall drawing. Record whether it is existing or proposed, the cover-plate reference and the person responsible for confirming its final position. Include grouped devices, switches near door openings and locations partly concealed by furniture. Ask the project team to reconcile the electrical and decorative elevations before approving a cutting plan. A photograph with a mark can support the discussion, but it should not replace a verified survey. Keep provisional positions clearly identified so the supplier does not mistake an early design sketch for permission to prepare fixed openings.

Separate the decorative surround from the electrical assembly

Barron Designs publishes different outlet-surround arrangements for its own faux-panel system, including recessed and more forward cover positions. Its instructions demonstrate that panel relief and the outlet face require coordination, and they flag electrical work for professional attention. Those details are not a Luvie installation specification and do not establish suitability for Uzbekistan. Ask the electrician and panel installer to agree the interface for the exact selected products. Do not prescribe an extender, fastening method or cut dimension from a foreign manufacturer's example. The supplier should clarify available decorative accessories, while the electrician determines the compliant electrical arrangement for the project.

Approve a mockup containing the actual visual relationship

Request a representative panel piece with the proposed opening treatment, surround and selected cover plate. A loose cover placed on an uncut face is useful for colour discussion but may not show the finished recess, edge or relief transition. Review the mockup from normal viewing positions and under lighting similar to the intended interior. Record the surrounding texture, visible cut edges, cover alignment and whether the design allows the electrician the required access. Document accepted materials and photographs against a drawing revision. The display should be non-energized: appearance review is not a live electrical test or a substitute for technical approval.

Allocate the work and control later substitutions

Separate panels, decorative surrounds, sample preparation and finishing labour in the quotation. Identify who supplies the electrical covers and who confirms their compatibility with the electrical equipment; these may be outside the panel supplier's scope. State who measures, approves the opening layout and deals with a changed cover model before cutting begins. If accessories will be purchased locally in Uzbekistan, confirm their references before treating the sample as final. Retain the approved mockup record for installation and handover. A change in panel relief, cover size or surround design should reopen the coordination review rather than being accepted as an equivalent item on colour alone.

Before you request a quotation

  • Outlet and switch references marked on a coordinated elevation.
  • Panel, cover and decorative surround references identified.
  • Non-energized mockup approved separately from electrical acceptance.
  • Supply, measurement, cutting approval and substitution responsibilities assigned.

Frequently asked questions

Can a decorative surround replace electrical approval?

No. It addresses a finish interface, not the safety or compliance of the electrical installation. Have the qualified project electrician review the electrical arrangement.

Should the supplier cut all openings from the first drawing?

Only after the responsible project team confirms the relevant positions and detail. Clearly label preliminary drawings and agree who authorizes the final cutting information.
