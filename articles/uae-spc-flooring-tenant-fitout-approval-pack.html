SPC Flooring in UAE Tenant Fitouts: Approval Pack

A tenant may select an SPC colour before the building manager has accepted the flooring proposal. For a UAE office or retail fitout, purchasing too early can leave the contractor with a material that does not match the agreed scope or submission requirements. The answer is not a larger brochure. It is a concise approval pack that separates product identification, installation design and the responsibilities of the parties reviewing it.

Short answer

Build the approval pack around the actual tenant space and product model. Track comments and revisions, and release procurement only when the required decisions are documented; product marketing is not building approval.

Ask what the building actually needs

Obtain the current fitout requirements from the landlord or building representative and identify who can accept the proposal. Different UAE buildings and uses can require different information, so avoid presenting one checklist as a universal legal rule. Ask which drawings, samples and product documents are expected and whether floor build-up or acoustic matters need specialist review. Record the submission deadline and any dependence on adjacent works. This helps the supplier provide relevant information without implying that a general catalogue satisfies every project requirement or that the supplier controls the approval timetable.

Identify one product and one proposed assembly

State the model, finish, dimensions and backing information supplied for the selected SPC product. Show the proposed substrate, any permitted underlay, transitions and perimeter conditions on the installation detail. Do not combine a test report for one product with a sample from another because the colours look similar. Where a document describes a tested assembly, keep its construction and limitations visible. If information is missing, mark it as a pending item and ask the supplier rather than filling the gap with a generic claim about all rigid-core flooring.

Keep comments attached to drawing revisions

A consultant comment about a doorway or underlay must reach the person preparing the purchase order. Use a short register with the comment, response, current revision and decision owner. Archive superseded drawings so a distributor does not quote from an earlier layout while the installer works from a later one. For an occupied building, include delivery and work-access constraints in the coordination record without treating them as product properties. A clear approval trail reduces the chance of buying the wrong accessory or promising an installation method that the responsible reviewer has not accepted.

Release the order with explicit boundaries

The final request should include the accepted finish and quantity basis, accessories, delivery destination and current drawing reference. Clarify whether installation, substrate preparation, testing and protection are included or separately procured. Manufacturer maintenance guidance such as the Tarkett example is useful context, but use instructions for the actual selected product in the handover pack. Approval of a submission does not eliminate the need for competent installation or site checks. Keep the accepted sample and documents available for receiving inspection, and record substitutions through the same approval route rather than approving them informally in a short message.

Before you request a quotation

  • Obtain the current building-specific submission requirements.
  • Match sample, model and supporting documents.
  • Track comments against a single current drawing revision.
  • Release the order with clear inclusions and unresolved items.

Frequently asked questions

Does a supplier catalogue count as fitout approval?

No. Acceptance comes through the applicable project and building review process.

Can an alternative backing be substituted after approval?

Do not assume so. Review the proposed change and its implications through the agreed approval process.
