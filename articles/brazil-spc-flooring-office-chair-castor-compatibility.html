SPC flooring for Brazil: coordinate office chair castors before buying

An office flooring purchase should not end with a colour selection and a floor-area calculation. Chairs bought by a different team may arrive with wheels that were never considered in the flooring enquiry. For Brazilian distributors and fit-out buyers, coordinating the floor, chair and any protective mat before approval creates a clearer specification and avoids relying on a general claim that a floor is suitable for commercial use.

Short answer

Ask the flooring supplier to review the exact chair castor and protective mat information against the proposed SPC model and intended use. Record the response and any conditions in the quotation approval. A wear-layer description or commercial-use label alone does not establish compatibility with every rolling chair, and another manufacturer's recommendation is not a Luvie product approval.

Build a chair schedule alongside the flooring schedule

Identify where rolling chairs will operate: individual desks, shared workstations, reception positions and meeting areas may have different patterns of use. Request the chair model, castor material and the supplier's current wheel specification rather than judging compatibility from a photograph. Include existing furniture that will remain after the refurbishment, not only newly ordered chairs. Name the person responsible for obtaining missing information. Where furniture selection is unfinished, mark compatibility as an open approval item instead of inserting a generic statement into the floor purchase order. This keeps the unresolved decision visible before material is committed.

Use the reference as a question, not a transferred guarantee

Tarkett Brazil's FAQ recommends polyurethane castors for its vinyl flooring and discusses scratching associated with unsuitable plastic wheels. That is useful evidence that wheel selection deserves attention, but it is guidance from another manufacturer. It does not certify a Luvie SPC construction, prescribe a universal wheel specification or establish a warranty entitlement. Send Luvie the selected flooring reference and the chair supplier's information, and ask for a written, model-specific response. If a report or test is offered, check the product identity, tested arrangement and stated limitations rather than treating the document title as approval for every project.

Approve a protective mat as a separate accessory

A chair mat is not an automatic solution to an unconfirmed flooring and castor combination. Ask both the mat and flooring suppliers whether the proposed backing, surface contact and intended placement are compatible with the selected finish. Obtain cleaning and maintenance instructions, and clarify whether a mat is required, permitted or unsuitable for this particular specification. Do not substitute an unrelated rug or rubber-backed product on site simply because it covers the same area. Record the exact approved accessory reference and who supplies it. Where a sample assembly is reviewed, document the materials and observations without presenting a short trial as long-term performance certification.

Carry the decision into purchasing and building operation

Attach the agreed compatibility response to the floor and furniture purchasing records so that one team cannot silently change a component after the other has approved it. Define who reviews substitutions and what information must accompany a replacement wheel or mat. At handover, give the facilities team the approved product references and maintenance documents, including any conditions supplied by the manufacturers. Agree how staff will identify and report damaged wheels, trapped debris or visible changes around workstations. Keep dated photographs and records if a concern develops, and consult the relevant supplier before changing the system. This is a practical coordination process, not a promise that damage can never occur.

Before you request a quotation

  • Flooring model and rolling-chair locations recorded.
  • Current castor specification supplied for review.
  • Protective mat reference and compatibility response documented.
  • Substitution approval and facilities handover responsibilities assigned.

Frequently asked questions

Does a thicker wear layer approve any office chair?

No. A wear-layer value does not by itself describe the complete flooring, wheel and use arrangement. Request confirmation for the exact proposed combination.

Can we approve the floor before choosing the chairs?

You can continue design discussions, but keep chair compatibility explicitly unresolved. Agree how that decision will be closed before the relevant purchase commitments are made.
