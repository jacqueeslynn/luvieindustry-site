SPC flooring for stairs in Peru: what to confirm before buying

A matching stair finish can make an interior feel coherent, but a plank approved for a flat floor is not automatically an approved stair system. Buyers in Peru should resolve the stair assembly before adding it to the flooring order.

Short answer

Request written confirmation that the exact SPC product and nosing assembly are suitable for the proposed stairs. Review fixing, substrate, edge geometry and local project requirements with the installer. Do not copy floating-floor instructions onto a staircase.

Treat the staircase as its own scope

List treads, risers, landings and exposed sides separately. Include photographs and measured drawings, noting irregular existing steps. Ask whether the supplier offers an approved matching nosing or whether a separate system must be sourced. If no suitable detail can be confirmed, choose another stair finish rather than improvising with ordinary floor trims.

Check the installation distinction

Karndean’s referenced rigid-core guide treats its residential stair installation as a special case requiring supplier advice. That does not approve any Luvie product for stairs. Use the example to ask the right questions: which components are fixed, which adhesive is approved, how is the edge secured, and what substrate preparation is required? Obtain a complete detail instead of isolated product recommendations.

Approve one representative step

Have a qualified installer build a representative sample where permitted. Review the nosing junction, exposed edges, finish alignment and cleanability. Ask the responsible project professional to check safety and applicable requirements, including the finished step geometry. A photograph is useful for appearance approval but cannot prove the security of concealed fixings or the suitability of the complete assembly.

Order by usable pieces

Count the actual pieces required for each tread and riser, then map cuts against available lengths. Include landings and any spare nosings in the schedule. For delivery in Peru, clarify whether replacement accessories can be supplied separately and record their identifiers. Keep approved sample references, installation documents and the final component list together for the handover.

Before you request a quotation

  • Measured stair drawing and intended building use.
  • Written suitability confirmation for the exact assembly.
  • Approved nosing, fixing and substrate detail.
  • Piece-by-piece cutting schedule and replacement identifiers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I glue any click SPC plank onto a stair?

Do not assume so. Obtain product-specific confirmation and an approved installation method.

Is a matching colour enough when choosing a nosing?

No. Geometry, fixing, compatibility and the project’s safety requirements are essential as well.
