SPC flooring in Brazil: hand over a usable retail cleaning plan

A Brazilian retailer buying SPC flooring needs more than an attractive sample and a delivery quotation. The people cleaning the shop after opening need to know which instructions apply to the selected floor, who approves a chemical change and when customers can use the area again. Put that information into the purchase handover rather than waiting for the first complaint about a slippery entrance or a changed surface appearance.

Short answer

Request the exact product care instructions, agree a practical cleaning trial and record responsibility for access control and reopening. A water-resistant material claim does not establish slip resistance or make a wet floor safe. Keep the cleaning method specific to the supplied floor and the actual shop operation.

Start with the floor identification, not a generic chemical list

Connect the maintenance sheet to the model, surface finish and supplied batch where available. Ask the supplier to identify permitted cleaning methods and restrictions in writing. Do not copy instructions from another SPC brand just because the boards look similar. Give the cleaning supervisor the same revision held by the store manager. If a proposed detergent or machine is not covered, obtain clarification before adding it to the routine. A vague instruction to use normal cleaner leaves different contractors making different decisions.

Separate product compatibility from pedestrian safety

The UK HSE explains that cleaning can leave a slip hazard and that a warning sign does not physically stop access. This is general operational guidance, not Brazilian legal advice or a slip rating for the selected floor. Plan how the retailer will separate the work area, maintain a usable customer route and decide when to reopen it. Have the responsible local team assess the actual conditions. Avoid promising that a textured finish eliminates the need for access management.

Run a controlled trial before routine cleaning starts

Agree a small representative trial with the installer and cleaning supervisor, using the proposed product, equipment and method only after compatibility is confirmed. Record the product name and the manufacturer-specified preparation instructions rather than inventing a universal dilution. Inspect the finish after it has dried under normal shop lighting. Note residue, appearance changes or difficult edges and obtain advice before repeating a problematic method across the store. Keep trial observations separate from formal performance testing; a satisfactory trial is not a certification.

Make the handover useful when staff or contractors change

Create a short Portuguese work instruction that names the approved reference documents and the person who can approve a change. Include the route for reporting damage, spills and missing instructions, and retain photographs of the accepted finished floor. For an entrance connected to a busy shopping corridor, agree cleaning responsibilities with the relevant property team rather than assuming the flooring supplier controls the public area. Keep a spare product reference for repair enquiries and review the handover when the cleaning contractor changes.

Before you request a quotation

  • Brazil destination, store use and selected flooring reference.
  • Current product-specific care instructions and unresolved compatibility questions.
  • A documented trial, access plan and responsible reopening decision.
  • Portuguese handover instructions and an identified maintenance contact.

Frequently asked questions

Does water resistance mean customers can walk safely on a wet floor?

No. Material resistance to water and pedestrian slip risk are different questions. Assess the actual floor, contamination and operating conditions.

Can the cleaning contractor substitute a familiar detergent?

Only after confirming compatibility with the selected product instructions and obtaining the agreed approval. Familiarity is not evidence of suitability.
