UV Marble Sheets in Iraq: Reception Desk Joint Layout

A reception desk has more corners, edges and contact points than a flat feature wall. Iraqi fitout buyers considering UV marble-look sheets should therefore approve the joint layout before treating the desk as a simple surface-area calculation. The most visible line may be a return beside the visitor, while the most heavily used edge may be on the staff side. Both deserve attention before cutting begins.

Short answer

Draw each desk face separately, identify exposed edges and establish which surfaces the proposed sheet is intended to cover. Do not assume wall-sheet suitability also covers worktops, unsupported spans or every corner detail.

Separate the decorative face from the working surface

Mark the front, side returns, staff-facing surfaces and any countertop on the desk drawing. A decorative wall sheet may be proposed only for selected vertical faces. Ask for written suitability information before including horizontal working surfaces or areas exposed to repeated contact, cleaners or hot objects. Specify the substrate and support arrangement separately from the decorative layer. The purchasing decision should follow the approved use, not the visual appeal of a marble pattern. If the designer intends a stone-like edge, request a buildable detail rather than assuming the sheet can be folded or fabricated like natural stone.

Position joints where the geometry permits

Use actual desk dimensions and supplier sheet dimensions to prepare the cutting layout. Consider whether the main visitor view should see a continuous face, a deliberate joint or a contrasting trim. Leave movement and installation allowances to the applicable product guidance; a drawing should not force sheets tightly together simply to hide a line. Include the desk’s returns, recesses and cable openings in the review. A small geometry change can alter the number of sheets required, so obtain the final approved drawing before comparing material quantities from different quotations.

Review pattern continuity at the actual scale

A catalogue thumbnail does not show how a large vein will cross a desk face or meet a side return. Ask for a layout using the offered pattern and clarify whether repeated sheets have the same image, variable faces or an available matching arrangement. Approve what is actually supplied rather than promise a bookmatch that has not been confirmed. Review the layout under the planned reception lighting, with the edge treatment visible. If the finish has a protective film, obtain instructions for its removal before judging the final gloss or colour. Record the accepted orientation and sheet reference.

Specify edge protection and handover information

Desk corners can be exposed to bags, cleaning tools and deliveries, but their actual duty depends on the project. Ask the fabricator to propose an edge detail compatible with the sheet and substrate, then review a sample corner. The quotation should separate decorative sheets, trims, fabrication, installation and any substrate work. General HSE handling guidance supports planning the movement of sheet materials; it does not specify the product’s impact resistance. Retain spare identification information and the approved cleaning method at handover. This is more useful than an unsupported promise that a glossy surface is maintenance-free.

Before you request a quotation

  • Mark every vertical and horizontal surface separately.
  • Approve a measured cutting and joint drawing.
  • Confirm pattern availability before promising matching veins.
  • Review a finished sample corner and cleaning instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can the same sheet automatically cover the countertop?

No. Confirm suitability for that use rather than extending a vertical-wall recommendation.

Does a marble-look pattern guarantee seamless bookmatching?

No. Matching depends on the offered pattern, available faces, layout and fabrication detail.
