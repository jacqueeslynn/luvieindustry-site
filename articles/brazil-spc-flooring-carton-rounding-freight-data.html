Brazil SPC flooring buyers: compare carton rounding before requesting freight

A quotation for 100 square metres does not identify a shipment. Brazilian importers need carton counts, pallet arrangements and gross packing mass. The named manufacturer example below explains a calculation method; it is not Luvie inventory or a specification for another model.

Short answer

Using the reference coverage of 2.15 m² per box, a hypothetical approved target of 100 m² requires 47 whole boxes, supplying 101.05 m². The extra 1.05 m² comes from rounding, not a recommended waste percentage. Gross shipping mass remains a separate input.

Start from an approved purchase area

Use the area already approved by the buyer and installer. Do not quietly add a generic waste percentage. Record cutting allowance and maintenance stock separately, with their basis. Here, 100 m² is a hypothetical purchase target, not a room measurement. Polyflor lists 2.15 m² per box, ten planks per box and forty boxes per pallet for Icelandic Oak 9025. Forty-six complete boxes fall short of the example target; forty-seven exceed it by 1.05 m².

A partial pallet is a question, not a promise

Forty-seven boxes can be separated mathematically into forty plus seven. This does not prove the supplier will dispatch one full pallet and one small pallet. Request the proposed pallet count, external dimensions, carton orientation and stackability. Do not substitute plank dimensions for packing dimensions. The actual arrangement can change the transport quotation.

Keep area and shipment mass in separate columns

Net product mass, gross carton mass and gross loaded-pallet mass are different inputs. A declared mass per square metre can support a net material calculation, but cannot establish the final gross shipment mass. Do not invent packaging or pallet weight. Ask the forwarder which measured weights and dimensions it needs for the proposed route and who will confirm the final packing list. This guide does not determine freight rates, taxes or Brazilian import approval.

Compare the actual order, not an artificial equal area

For two alternative models, calculate whole-box quantities independently using each current packing table. Comparing prices for exactly 100 m² can hide rounding and packaging differences. Keep the approved installation allowance separate from surplus caused by complete-box ordering. Ask how incomplete pallets are handled before booking transport, and confirm whether the quoted packing configuration applies to your actual order.

Before you request a quotation

  • Identify the model and current packing revision.
  • Separate project area, cutting allowance and maintenance stock.
  • Confirm the ordering unit before rounding.
  • Request gross weights and external packing dimensions.
  • Confirm the arrangement for incomplete pallets.

Frequently asked questions

Does 1.05 m² extra cover installation waste?

Not necessarily. It is only the rounding difference in this example; layout and cutting determine the installation allowance.

Can forty boxes per pallet be used for another SPC model?

No. Obtain the selected model's current packing data.

Can net flooring mass serve as the final freight weight?

No. Request the gross packing weights and dimensions required by the forwarder.
