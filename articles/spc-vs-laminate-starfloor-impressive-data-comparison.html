SPC versus laminate: what Starfloor Ultimate 55 and Impressive data actually show

Is SPC better than laminate? A professional purchasing comparison starts with named products, not generic ranges. Here the examples are Tarkett Starfloor Click Ultimate 55 and Quick-Step Impressive, reference IMXXXX, technical-sheet edition 01.2026. Tarkett's Italian specification identifies its example as SPC with integrated acoustic backing; the international literature also uses the proprietary RCC description. Quick-Step identifies its example as laminate with an HDF core. These are external reference products, not Luvie models, and this comparison does not imply that Luvie supplies either brand or matches their performance. IMXXXX is the manufacturer's family reference pattern, not an individual décor SKU; confirm the chosen décor separately.

Short answer

The published nominal thicknesses are 6.50 mm and 8.0 mm respectively. That is a useful starting point for floor-height coordination, not a durability ranking. Published thermal resistance values are numerically close but use different referenced standards. Acoustic results depend on assembly and documentation. A missing comparable result means not established, not zero or inferior. Select against the actual project requirements and obtain current model-specific evidence before ordering.

Use geometry for quantities, not a quality score

The table preserves declared pack coverage rather than presenting calculated rounding as a new manufacturer specification. For the same measured room, different plank formats can produce different cutting layouts, so neither pack coverage nor plank size alone determines the final order. Prepare a layout, identify permitted installation direction and agree the cutting allowance for that design. Then round the resulting requirement to the offered pack configuration. A thinner board may help at a constrained doorway, but the complete installed build-up includes whatever layers the selected system requires. Never remove a required layer just to make the arithmetic fit.

Do not turn unlike durability measurements into a league table

The SPC sheet gives a PVC wear-layer thickness. That describes a layer dimension under its stated method; it is not a promise of a particular service life. Laminate surface-performance evidence belongs to a different construction and testing framework. Do not convert millimetres of PVC into a laminate abrasion class or claim that the thicker overall board automatically resists scratches better. Request the property that matters to the intended use, together with its test method, result and configuration. If the two offers do not report comparable evidence, identify the gap and ask for clarification instead of filling it with an industry-wide range.

Read thermal and acoustic numbers with their conditions

A small numerical difference in thermal resistance does not establish a heating-cost saving. These sheets cite different standards, and the project designer needs the resistance of the actual installed assembly, including any required underlay. The acoustic row is an even clearer warning against casual ranking: the laminate result varies with the underlay, and its sheet prints a method designation that needs manufacturer clarification. We preserve that notation rather than silently correcting it. Ask for the relevant test report and assembly description before using either value in a building acoustic assessment. A laboratory improvement figure is not a prediction of the sound heard in every room.

Know when SPC is the wrong purchasing shortcut

Do not select SPC to avoid unresolved subfloor moisture, inadequate flatness, an incompatible heating arrangement or a project requirement that lacks supporting evidence. A rigid core is not permission to ignore preparation. For the named Tarkett example, the sheet limits underfloor-heating suitability to a maximum subfloor temperature of 27°C; this is not a universal limit for every SPC product. If the proposed assembly cannot satisfy its current instructions, choose a compliant alternative or redesign the build-up with the responsible professional. Laminate may remain a viable candidate where its own conditions are met. Neither material should be approved simply because the other is unsuitable.

Before you request a quotation

  • Confirm exact model, market and current document revision.
  • Compare complete installed height and layout-based pack quantities.
  • Obtain matching methods and assembly descriptions for critical performance.
  • Resolve substrate, heating and installation restrictions before purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Does 8 mm laminate outlast 6.5 mm SPC?

The stated thicknesses alone cannot answer that. Construction, surface tests, installation, traffic and maintenance differ; request evidence for the property and use that matter.

Can these figures be entered as Luvie specifications?

No. They belong to the named external products. Ask Luvie for the current offered model's documentation and evaluate it independently against the same project requirements.
