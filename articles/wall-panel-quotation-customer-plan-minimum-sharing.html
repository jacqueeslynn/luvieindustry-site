Wall-panel quotations: prepare a limited customer-plan extract before sharing

A distributor may receive a complete customer drawing set when only one wall needs pricing. Forwarding the entire attachment can expose unrelated room layouts, personal contact details or confidential notes that the panel supplier does not need. Removing everything, however, can make a quotation unreliable by hiding the dimensions or interfaces that matter. The practical task is to prepare a purpose-specific extract that preserves useful technical information while limiting unnecessary disclosure. This is a procurement workflow, not legal advice or a guarantee that a file is anonymous.

Short answer

Define the quotation question, confirm authority to share, then create a separate extract containing only the relevant wall, dimensions and finish references. Remove unrelated identifiers and hidden content using an appropriate document process, check the resulting file and record who receives it. Keep the unmodified original within the customer's authorized project records.

Independent reference

Start with the supplier's actual information need

Ask what the supplier must evaluate: a product family, estimated quantity, a junction or a proposed sheet layout. List the information needed for that decision before preparing the attachment. A wall identifier, dimensions, openings and a finish code may be enough for an initial discussion, while another question may require additional context. Do not erase a relevant opening merely because it belongs to another trade. Where scale or orientation matters, preserve it explicitly. Mark the extract as quotation information and identify unresolved dimensions so the recipient does not mistake an incomplete discussion document for an approved fabrication drawing.

Confirm permission and remove unrelated identifiers

Check the customer's sharing instructions and your own authority before sending a drawing outside the project team. The UK ICO describes data minimisation as keeping personal information adequate, relevant and limited to the purpose. That principle is a useful reference here, not a determination of which country's law applies to your transaction. Seek appropriate advice when necessary. Consider whether names, personal telephone numbers, signatures, occupied-room labels or unrelated security details serve the quotation purpose. Replace unnecessary project names with a controlled reference where suitable, but do not claim that changing a name alone makes the plan anonymous.

Create and inspect a separate sharing file

Work on a copy and use a document process appropriate to the file type. A black rectangle placed over text may only conceal it visually; it should not be assumed to remove the underlying information. Check comments, attachments, layers, metadata and content outside the intended drawing area where the format can contain them. Review the final exported file rather than only the editing view. Confirm that intended technical information remains readable and that removed material is not recoverable through ordinary selection or search. For sensitive drawings, involve the organization's document or security specialist instead of improvising a redaction method.

Control delivery and the later revision trail

Send the approved extract only to the intended recipient through the organization's accepted sharing method. Check the address or access list, and avoid broad links that grant more access than the quotation needs. Record the reference, revision, purpose and recipient so the next inquiry does not automatically reuse an unrestricted original. If the supplier needs more detail, review that request and provide a further approved extract rather than forwarding the whole archive. Follow the customer's and organization's retention instructions; do not promise deletion that you cannot verify. If a wrong file is sent, promptly use the established incident process and notify the responsible internal contact.

Before you request a quotation

  • Quotation purpose and necessary technical context defined.
  • Authority to share and unnecessary identifiers reviewed.
  • Separate exported extract inspected for visible and hidden content.
  • Recipient access, revision record and retention instructions confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Does removing the customer's name make a floor plan anonymous?

Not necessarily. Addresses, distinctive layouts, annotations and other details may still identify a person or project. Describe what was removed accurately and obtain specialist review where the sensitivity requires it.

Should every dimension be removed for privacy?

No. A useful extract must still support its stated technical purpose. Share only the relevant dimensions and context with authorization, and distinguish preliminary quotation information from approved production instructions.
