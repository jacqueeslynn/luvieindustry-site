Importing PVC panels into Brazil: verify a change of payment details before releasing funds

A PVC panel order may already have an agreed finish, carton plan and commercial invoice when a new email asks the Brazilian buyer to pay a different account. Familiar logos and a convincing order reference are not sufficient evidence that the instruction is genuine. A compromised conversation can contain accurate commercial details. Treat a change of beneficiary or payment procedure as a separate verification event, even when the material order itself has not changed. This guide concerns purchasing controls, not Brazilian banking or tax advice.

Short answer

Pause the changed payment instruction, verify it through an independently established contact channel and route the documented result through your finance approval process. Keep the product order separate from the beneficiary-change decision. Never use a telephone number supplied only in the suspicious message as the verification channel.

Recognize the change that needs a new decision

Compare the new instruction with the approved supplier record, not merely with the previous message in the same email conversation. Identify whether the beneficiary name, account, bank, currency, payment destination or payment procedure has changed. Save the received instruction with its date and order reference, following your internal security policy. Do not forward sensitive bank information into a general project chat. For a PVC shipment, the finish reference, panel quantity and packing details can remain correct while payment instructions are fraudulent. Conversely, a genuine administrative change still needs approval. Avoid concluding either fraud or authenticity from formatting alone. Record precisely what is different and who is responsible for checking it.

Verify outside the disputed conversation

The FBI recommends independently verifying payment changes and contacting the company using a number found independently rather than one supplied by a possible scammer. Apply that general safeguard to the order in front of you: use a previously verified supplier contact from your controlled records and ask your finance team to follow its authentication procedure. The FBI source is fraud-prevention guidance, not a determination about a particular supplier or Brazilian banking requirement. A reply from the same mailbox is not an independent check if that mailbox might be compromised. If the established contact cannot be reached, keep the changed instruction on hold and escalate internally rather than treating silence as consent.

Keep order approval and payment authority separate

A sales contact can explain the PVC model and commercial documents without being the person authorized to approve a new beneficiary in your organization. Record who requested the change, who independently confirmed it and who approved the finance action. Refer to your current supplier-onboarding and payment-control rules instead of improvising an exception because a shipment is urgent. Check that the verified documents belong to the same legal counterparty as the transaction being approved. Resolve any mismatch through the responsible finance personnel. Do not invent an alternative routing arrangement or split a transfer simply to bypass an unresolved check. Commercial deadlines should be discussed separately; urgency does not establish the authenticity of an account.

Close the verification with a usable audit trail

Store the decision in the controlled order record with the relevant document version, verification date and authorized reviewer. Limit access to sensitive information according to company policy. Tell the purchasing team which instruction is currently approved without circulating unnecessary account details. If a payment has already been sent and compromise is suspected, contact your bank immediately through its established channel and follow its incident process; recovery cannot be promised. Review any connected orders that use the same supplier record so the next buyer does not unknowingly rely on the disputed change. This final check is about the payment workflow, not evidence that the panels meet a technical specification or that shipment release has occurred.

Before you request a quotation

  • Identify the exact changed fields against the approved supplier record.
  • Use an independently established contact channel for verification.
  • Record the finance approver separately from product-order approval.
  • Keep unresolved instructions on hold and preserve the decision trail.

Frequently asked questions

Does a matching invoice logo prove the new account is genuine?

No. Logos and order details can be copied. Use your independent verification and finance approval process rather than appearance as evidence.

Should we send account information through the website inquiry form?

No. Use the inquiry form for product requirements. Exchange sensitive payment documentation only through your established, appropriately controlled business process.
