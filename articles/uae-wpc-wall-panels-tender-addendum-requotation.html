UAE WPC wall panels: turn a tender addendum into a comparable revised quotation

A tender update can change much more than the total area of WPC wall panels. A revised elevation may introduce a different profile, add returns around openings or replace a finish in only part of the project. If a buyer forwards the new drawing without identifying the changes, competing quotations may price different scopes. For a UAE interior procurement package, the useful output is a reconciled change schedule that lets the buyer compare the earlier quotation with the new one. This is a purchasing workflow, not a statement of local tender law.

Short answer

Issue one identified addendum package with a line-by-line list of added, removed and changed requirements. Ask the supplier to acknowledge the exact revision and show the commercial effect separately from unchanged items. Do not accept a revised grand total until the model, finish, quantity units, accessories and exclusions have been reconciled.

Start with the priced baseline

Identify the quotation number and the drawing, specification and quantity schedule on which it was based. Record the actual WPC profile reference, finish reference and proposed length rather than a broad description such as wood-look wall panel. Confirm whether quantities were stated as panels, packs or area and how any conversion was calculated. Keep this baseline available throughout the comparison. Without it, a new quote may look cheaper simply because one party has omitted trims or priced a different quantity basis. The baseline is a reference for comparison, not automatic permission to order a superseded configuration. Mark it accordingly so the site or purchasing team does not mistake the comparison copy for the current release.

Describe each changed requirement

Build a change table with the original requirement, revised requirement, affected room or elevation and controlling document reference. Separate a new finish from a new profile; similar photographs do not establish that the same tooling, dimensions or accessory arrangement is proposed. If an area increases, identify whether the new area uses an existing reference or introduces another combination. Ask the responsible design team to resolve unclear transitions and exposed edges before the supplier assumes a detail. Request a quote that exposes the differences

Ask for the revised quantities and units alongside the corresponding product references. Identify additions, deletions and unchanged lines separately; a supplier may reasonably need to reassess packing or other commercial terms, but those effects should be explicit. Confirm whether existing accessories remain appropriate when the panel configuration changes. Request a clear statement of exclusions and unresolved assumptions rather than accepting a blanket note that the price is subject to drawings. Compare currency, commercial basis and the scope of any delivery allowance consistently. This guide does not provide a price, lead time or minimum order. Obtain those terms from the actual quotation and avoid interpreting a previous offer as a current commitment.

Close the addendum before ordering

Record the supplier acknowledgement of the issued revision and obtain internal approval of the reconciled scope. Acknowledging receipt alone does not confirm that every change has been priced. Resolve exceptions in writing and link the final quotation to the accepted addendum package. Before releasing the purchase order, check the panel references, finish allocation, quantities and accessory lines against that accepted version. Archive earlier comparisons without deleting their history, while making the current purchase basis easy to identify. If another revision arrives, reopen only the affected comparison with an explicit decision on the remaining scope. This prevents a running conversation from becoming an accidental mixture of several tender versions.

Before you request a quotation

  • Identify the original priced document set.
  • Separate profile, finish, quantity and accessory changes.
  • Obtain line-level revised scope and explicit exclusions.
  • Link the accepted quotation and purchase order to the same addendum.

Frequently asked questions

Is a supplier email saying received enough?

It establishes receipt, not complete pricing acceptance. Ask the supplier to identify included changes and any exceptions before the buyer approves the revised offer.

Can we compare only the new total?

That can conceal changed quantities or exclusions. Reconcile the product and commercial scope first, then compare the totals on the same basis.
