PU stone-effect walls in Qatar: check curtain movement beside textured relief

A wall finish and a curtain can each be approved independently yet interfere when installed together. For a Qatar fit-out buyer, the risk is easy to miss when the panel is judged flat on a table and the curtain is represented only by a fabric swatch. Raised stone-effect detail occupies space, while the curtain changes shape as it opens and gathers. The useful procurement task is to review that moving interface before both packages are fixed, not to assume a universal gap will suit every panel and fabric.

Short answer

Ask the curtain specialist and wall designer to review the actual panel profile, track position and fabric arrangement together. Test a representative assembly through its intended movement and record any contact or snagging. Agree a project-specific detail before ordering; this guide gives no standard clearance and does not claim that any Luvie surface is snag-proof.

Describe the moving curtain, not only the fabric

Request the proposed track reference, heading arrangement, lining and intended opening direction. Identify where the fabric gathers when fully open and whether it returns toward the wall at either end. A sample swatch establishes neither the volume of those folds nor their path during use. Silent Gliss provides curtain-planning guidance for its own systems, which illustrates why window treatment needs its own coordinated design. Its information is not a clearance specification for Luvie panels. Ask the selected curtain supplier for the details relevant to the actual project rather than borrowing dimensions from a different track system.

Measure from the proposed finished wall profile

Give the designer a representative panel sample and current model information. Distinguish the existing wall plane from the proposed finished face, including any approved backing and the relief that projects toward the fabric. Identify exposed ends and corners near the curtain path, not just the broad central surface. Confirm which trade owns the junction detail and who can approve moving the track or changing the extent of the cladding. Do not let an installer trim or sand a decorative face as an improvised clearance solution without appropriate product advice and project approval. Such work could change the approved appearance or surface condition.

Trial the interface through normal use

Arrange a representative mock-up with the actual fabric construction and the proposed wall profile. Open, close and gather the curtain using its intended operating method, with the responsible trades present. Observe whether folds touch raised areas, whether the hem drifts toward an edge and whether fabric catches during the movement. Stop rather than pulling harder if resistance appears. Record the configuration and observations; a brief trial is not a lifetime durability guarantee. Where the design includes two layers, review their combined movement instead of checking each alone. Reassess after changes to lining, heading, track position or panel reference.

Close the order with a coordinated acceptance record

Record the accepted arrangement in both purchasing packages so that a later substitution does not preserve only half of the design. Identify the approved wall extent, curtain assembly and the people who accepted the trial. If clearance requires a smooth return or a change in track position, have the designer resolve it and ask the relevant supplier to confirm the proposed detail. Keep cleaning and maintenance access in view so the curtain can be removed or serviced without forcing it past the relief. At installation handover, repeat the movement check and record any unresolved contact before accepting the interface.

Before you request a quotation

  • Track, heading, lining and open-curtain gathering positions identified.
  • Finished wall profile, exposed ends and corners reviewed together.
  • Representative movement trial completed with both trades.
  • Accepted detail, substitution controls and handover check recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Can the buyer specify one curtain-to-wall gap for every project?

Not from this guide. Panel relief, backing, curtain construction and track arrangement differ. Ask the responsible designer and curtain specialist to set and approve the relevant project detail.

Does no snagging in a short trial prove long-term performance?

No. It provides observations about that particular assembly and movement. It does not certify wear resistance or eliminate the need for installation checks, care instructions and review after substitutions.

Next step

Send Luvie the selected PU panel reference and a drawing showing its relationship to the curtain. Request current profile and care information. Coordinate the moving fabric with the curtain specialist before confirming the wall-finish order, keeping any claimed performance limited to available evidence.
