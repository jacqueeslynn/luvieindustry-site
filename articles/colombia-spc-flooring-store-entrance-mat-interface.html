SPC Store Entrances in Colombia: Detail the Mat Edge

A Colombian shop entrance receives a different pattern of traffic and tracked-in material from a quiet display area. When SPC flooring meets an entrance mat, the buyer should coordinate the boundary rather than assuming the installer can solve it with any available strip. The important decisions include levels, mat retention, cleaning and the floor system’s installation requirements.

Short answer

Specify the entrance as a complete interface. Have the designer and installer confirm the mat arrangement and transition detail for the actual floor, while keeping accessibility and slip requirements with the local project team.

Independent reference

NIST: SI units. General guidance, not a Luvie product certification.

Survey levels and the operating door

Record the existing floor, proposed finished build-up, door movement and any recess intended for the mat. Include adjacent thresholds and the direction of travel. Use consistent units in the survey and material schedule; NIST provides a general SI unit reference, not a standard transition height for this project. Mark any uncertainty that requires site measurement. A thin-looking product sample does not establish the final level once preparation, backing and the complete installed arrangement are included.

Choose a mat arrangement before ordering the transition

Ask whether the operator intends a recessed mat, a loose mat or another system, and have the responsible designer assess it. Confirm the mat manufacturer’s instructions and the flooring manufacturer’s compatibility guidance, including any restrictions relevant to backing materials. Do not assume that every rubber-backed mat or adhesive is compatible with every decorative floor. The transition should retain its intended function without obstructing required movement or creating an unreviewed edge. Product selection and safe access need to be considered together rather than as separate last-minute purchases.

Include cleaning in the design discussion

Ask how the entrance will be cleaned, how the mat is removed and where collected dirt or moisture goes during maintenance. Avoid promising that a floor category eliminates slip risk or water-related issues. Discuss the actual exposure with the supplier and contractor, and obtain product-specific care guidance. A trial detail can help the operator understand access and appearance, but it is not a performance test. If the entrance conditions exceed the proposed product’s intended use, the project team should reconsider the material rather than obscure the limitation.

Price the interface as a complete scope

List flooring, transition components, mat system and any locally supplied preparation separately. Confirm who verifies final dimensions and who installs each part. Keep approved details with the order so a different trim is not substituted solely because it is available on the day. At handover, retain maintenance instructions for both the mat and floor. This provides a practical buying route for the Colombian retailer without inventing a universal clearance, safety classification or guarantee that any SPC product suits any entrance.

Before you request a quotation

  • Survey finished levels and door operation.
  • Choose the mat system before transition components.
  • Check backing compatibility and maintenance access.
  • Assign responsibility for every part of the interface.

Frequently asked questions

Can any loose mat be placed on SPC flooring?

Check the exact floor and mat guidance; backing compatibility and safe placement cannot be assumed.

Does a waterproof label mean the entrance cannot be slippery?

No. Slip behaviour and entrance safety require separate assessment of actual conditions and applicable requirements.
