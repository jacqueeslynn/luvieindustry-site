## Calculations and editorial limits

- Product thickness difference5.5−2.0=3.5mm; not installed assembly thickness difference.
- Mass difference8570−3600=4970g/m²=4.97kg/m². At hypothetical100m²:857kg versus360kg, difference497kg. Excludes packaging, waste, installation components.
- Acoustic arithmetic18−2=16dB, explicitly NOT field room reduction, NOT percentage. Same method citation does not establish same tested assembly.
- Keep < versus ≤. Declared average bounds do NOT support exact two-times indentation claim.
- Abrasion cycles versus GroupT are noncomparable reporting bases. Do not rank them numerically.
- No Luvie specifications, certifications, stock, prices, installation approval, lifetime or market-wide ranges inferred. No claim of partnership with Polyflor.
- `sources` array contains all five links for publisher to render; `comparison` holds caption/headers/rows per locale. Section text plain because publisher escapes HTML.
