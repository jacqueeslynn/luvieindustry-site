## English working draft

# Brazil SPC flooring buyers: compare carton rounding before requesting freight

A quotation for 100 square metres does not identify a shipment. A Brazilian importer still needs a carton count, pallet arrangement and gross mass before asking a forwarder for a usable freight quotation. Compare those fields separately: product area is not packaging volume, and net flooring mass is not shipping weight. The calculation below uses one named manufacturer's packing table to explain the method, not to advertise that product as Luvie inventory.

## Start from the approved purchase area

Use the area already approved by the buyer and installer. Do not quietly add a generic waste percentage. If the project requires cutting allowance or maintenance stock, record those quantities separately and explain how they were determined. Here, 100 m² is a hypothetical approved purchase target, not a room measurement or an allowance recommendation.

Polyflor's Icelandic Oak 9025 page lists 2.15 m² per box, ten planks per box and forty boxes per pallet. Dividing the hypothetical target by the listed box coverage gives 100 / 2.15 = 46.5116 boxes. If ordering complete boxes, forty-six boxes fall short; forty-seven provide 101.05 m². The additional 1.05 m² is a packaging-rounding consequence, not a universal waste allowance.

## Comparison worksheet

| Measure | Calculation or reference | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Approved target | Hypothetical 100 m² | Already includes any separately approved allowance |
| Box coverage | Published 2.15 m² | Named reference model only |
| Whole-box order | Round 46.5116 up to 47 | Confirm supplier accepts this quantity |
| Supplied area | 47 × 2.15 = 101.05 m² | Do not relabel rounding as installation waste |
| Full-pallet coverage | 40 × 2.15 = 86 m² | Reference packing configuration |
| Remaining boxes | 47 − 40 = 7 | Does not establish how the remainder will ship |
| Gross shipment mass | Not supplied in this reference table | Request actual carton and pallet packing data |

## A partial pallet is a question, not a shipping promise

Forty-seven boxes are mathematically one forty-box group plus seven boxes. That does not prove the supplier will dispatch one full pallet and one small pallet. The remainder might require a different arrangement, and loading restrictions may change the practical plan. Ask for pallet count, external dimensions, carton orientation and stackability confirmation for the exact proposed shipment. Do not estimate freight using the product's plank dimensions as though those were pallet dimensions.

## Compare like-for-like freight inputs

Keep net product mass, gross carton mass and gross loaded-pallet mass in different columns. If only net mass per square metre is available, it can support a material calculation but not a final gross shipment declaration. Packaging and pallet mass must not be invented. Ask the forwarder which measured weights and dimensions it needs for the proposed route, and which party will confirm the final packing list. This article does not quote freight, tax, customs classification or Brazilian import approval.

## Turn the worksheet into a useful inquiry

Send Luvie the selected SPC model, approved purchase area, destination in Brazil and whether maintenance stock is separate. Ask for confirmed box coverage, available whole-box quantity, proposed pallet arrangement and gross packing information. If two alternatives use different box coverage, calculate each order independently. Comparing only the price of exactly 100 m² can conceal the effect of whole-box rounding and different packaging.

### Before requesting a quotation

- Identify the exact model and current packing revision.
- Separate project area, cutting allowance and maintenance stock.
- Round quantities only after confirming the ordering unit.
- Request actual gross weights and external packing dimensions.
- Confirm how incomplete pallets are handled before booking transport.

### Does 1.05 m² extra mean the installation allowance is sufficient?

No. It is only the difference caused by rounding the hypothetical order to complete reference boxes. Installation allowance depends on the approved layout and cutting plan.

### Can I use forty boxes per pallet for another SPC model?

No. The figure belongs to the named reference packing table. Obtain the proposed model's current packing data.

### Can I quote freight from net flooring mass alone?

Not as a final shipment specification. Obtain the gross packing information and external dimensions required by your forwarder.
