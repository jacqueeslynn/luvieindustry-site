### Users
International importers, distributors and project buyers comparing decorative wall and floor materials. They need to understand product fit, inspect evidence, open catalogs and contact Luvie with a specification-ready inquiry.

### Brand Personality
Professional, warm and dependable. The site should feel like a considered building-material supplier rather than a generic marketing template.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the beige-and-brown visual direction requested by the site owner. Keep warm ivory and sand surfaces, dark brown text, restrained clay-brown accents and the existing gold-toned brand identity. Avoid conspicuous cool grey blocks, purple/blue gradients, heavy glass effects and stock-looking visual claims. English pages and downloadable buyer-facing catalogs should not show Chinese copy unless explicitly labeled as source material.

### Design Principles
1. Make product, catalog and inquiry routes easy to identify without reading the whole homepage.
2. Use warm neutrals consistently across sections, cards and overlays; reserve stronger brown for priority actions.
3. Clearly label illustrations and concepts; do not present them as documented factory or customer-project evidence.
4. Keep English copy concise and visually scannable on desktop and mobile.
5. Maintain readable contrast and visible keyboard focus for links and controls.

### Quality Bar and Review Loop
Treat Awwwards, The Webby Awards and FWA as a benchmark for craft, not as an award claim. Before each major release, inspect the live homepage, product path, article, catalog path and inquiry flow at desktop and phone widths in all four languages. Check visual hierarchy, authentic imagery, typography, navigation, motion restraint, accessibility, loading and conversion clarity. Fix the largest observed issue, capture a new view, and repeat; never call the site award-ready based on a code-only review or an unverified deployment. Preserve the site's B2B buying utility while improving visual distinctiveness.
