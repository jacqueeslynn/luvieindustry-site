Direct buyer answer

A comparable SPC flooring quote should identify plank size, total thickness, wear layer, core and attached underlay if any, click profile, finish, carton coverage, accessories and destination-specific test documents. Ask for the exact construction in writing.

Start with the installation setting

Residential and light-commercial projects may prioritize different wear, cleaning and replacement needs. The substrate condition, moisture assessment, expansion detail and installer instructions are part of the flooring system. Do not assume that an SPC plank corrects an uneven or damp subfloor.

Use our SPC distributor specification guide to prepare a side-by-side shortlist. It separates product attributes from site preparation and market-specific compliance.

Approve color, locking and paperwork

Request a joined sample, not only one loose plank. Check color under destination lighting and keep a labeled approved reference. Ask for the relevant product test report when the destination or project requires one; the test method and named SKU must match what is ordered.

Price, minimum order quantity, available finishes and delivery schedule are order-specific and should be confirmed in a written quotation.
