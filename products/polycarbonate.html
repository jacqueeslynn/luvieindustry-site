Compare the five formats

Solid polycarbonate sheets

A continuous solid section for glazing and protective partitions. Confirm thickness, optical finish and the supporting frame before selection.

Corrugated polycarbonate sheets

A profiled sheet for daylight roof sections. Match the wave profile, effective cover width and overlaps to the adjoining roofing; similar-looking profiles are not automatically compatible.

U-lock solid polycarbonate system

Solid panels with raised locking edges, specified together with their connectors. Review the complete joint, edge closure and support detail rather than ordering the sheet alone.

7-layer interlocking polycarbonate panels

A seven-layer cellular panel with interlocking edges for coordinated translucent envelopes. Obtain the actual section drawing and system details: layer count alone does not establish thermal or structural performance.

Multiwall polycarbonate sheets

A hollow cellular section for translucent roof and wall assemblies. Coordinate channel direction, end treatment and connectors with the chosen system; confirm the exact wall geometry in the technical drawing.

Build your project specification

  1. Application and destination: roof, facade, partition or glazing; project location and required approvals.
  2. Panel schedule: section type, thickness, cut length, effective width, quantity, colour and transparency.
  3. System details: support spacing, joints, fixings, end closures, drainage and movement allowances for engineering review.
  4. Evidence and order terms: request a current drawing, sample, model-specific test documents, packing plan and written quotation.
