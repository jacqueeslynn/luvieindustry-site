Direct buyer answer

Ask for the board substrate, nominal and usable dimensions, thickness, surface construction, finish reference, edge and joint detail, recommended fixing method and packing for the selected sheet. A marble-look sheet is a decorative surface, not a substitute for waterproofing or a heat-rated lining.

Match the surface to the room

Large-format sheets can create a continuous visual field in a dry interior, but seams, corners, service openings and substrate flatness still determine the installed result. In humid or heat-exposed rooms, ask for product-specific suitability and installation evidence before specifying the panel.

Our UV marble sheet buyer guide explains why the substrate and coating must be checked separately. The heat-exposure guide sets out the questions to ask near cooking equipment and direct sunlight.

Keep claims tied to the test report

“Waterproof,” “fireproof,” “heat resistant” and “low VOC” need definitions and product-specific evidence. Request the current report and identify its named material, test method, thickness and mounting conditions. The standards and evidence guide shows how to review a report without treating an industry standard as a certificate for every sheet.
