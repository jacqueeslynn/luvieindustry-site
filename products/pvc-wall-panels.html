Direct buyer answer

A useful PVC wall panel shortlist specifies the exact model, effective coverage after joining, thickness, usable length, finish, compatible trims, fixing method and intended room conditions. Ask for product-specific documents where your market or project requires them.

Choose the PVC panel by application

For a feature wall, the finish, joint rhythm and edge detail may drive the decision. For a full-room renovation, ease of cleaning, transition details and replacement stock may matter more. In humid rooms, the panel face is only one part of the assembly: joints, penetrations, substrate condition and ventilation must also be planned.

Do not treat every product in a PVC catalog as a ceiling panel or as a waterproof wall system. Ask whether the quoted profile is intended for the proposed position and how it should be supported. Read the humid-area buyer guide before specifying a bathroom or wet-adjacent application.

Sample and evidence checks before purchase

Request joined samples, a cross-section, a current product drawing and a trim sample. Check the face and seam under the lighting used in the destination space. If a test report is required, verify that its named product and mounting conditions match the selected SKU; a report for another panel does not certify this one.

The U.S. EPA moisture-control guidance explains why water management is a whole-building issue. It does not establish the performance of any Luvie product. For commercial projects, use the standards and evidence guide to organize document requests.

Turn a shortlist into a comparable inquiry

Send your destination country, application, room dimensions, finish reference, estimated quantity and target order timing. Add drawings and installation photos where possible. Ask Luvie to identify relevant PVC models, connected coverage, compatible accessories, packing details and available documents for the chosen product. Price, minimum order quantity and lead time must be confirmed for the exact order; they are not fixed by this page.

When comparing PVC with another material, use the PVC versus WPC buyer comparison. For quantity planning, start with the wall panel order calculation guide, then apply the confirmed PVC profile dimensions.
