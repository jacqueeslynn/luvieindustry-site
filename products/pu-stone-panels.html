Direct buyer answer

Request the exact SKU, dimensions, profile and relief depth, intended location, fixing instructions, compatible trims, packing and current product documents. Do not assume every stone-look panel is suitable outdoors or meets a destination fire requirement.

Begin with the intended location

An indoor accent wall, sheltered entrance and exposed facade involve different conditions. Ask whether the selected PU panel is specified for the proposed exposure and substrate. For outdoor work, review sunlight, water paths, wind loading and local approval requirements with the project team.

For the material comparison, read our PU stone versus natural stone guide. It explains questions about weight, surface appearance, fixing and long-term maintenance without claiming equivalence to natural stone.

Approve the physical reference before production

Photographs do not establish the final color or relief. Label the approved sample with the model and finish, then carry that reference into the order specification and inspection criteria. Ask how replacement material or future batches will be identified.

Price, minimum order quantity, available finishes, warranty and lead time depend on the selected SKU and order. They should be stated in the written quotation, not assumed from a general catalog.

Build a comparable PU stone inquiry

Send the destination country, indoor or outdoor location, substrate, approximate area, finish reference and estimated order volume. We can discuss the matching sample and documents.
