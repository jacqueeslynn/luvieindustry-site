# Company white paper: publication evidence gate

The existing 10-page *Luvie Industry Company & Sourcing White Paper 2026* is a draft, not an approved public asset. Its source file is outside this repository at `outputs/Luvie_Industry_Company_White_Paper_2026.pdf` in the current Codex workspace. Do not upload or link it from the site until the following claims have been checked against current company records and approved by Luvie.

| Draft claim | Evidence needed before publication | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 years of wall-panel manufacturing experience | Company timeline showing the entity, facility and relevant production start date | Unverified in this repository |
| Own factory in Haining | Legal entity/facility relationship and dated real site photographs | Unverified in this repository |
| Products sold to 50+ countries and regions | An auditable country list or export summary defining the time period and counting method | Unverified in this repository |
| ISO 9001-certified quality management system | Current certificate, legal entity, issuing body, scope and expiry date | Unverified in this repository |
| 90% of orders delivered on time | Order-level calculation, period, promised-date definition and exclusions | Unverified in this repository |
| Replies begin within 24 hours | Service owner and a sustainable operating process or measured response-time record | Unverified in this repository |
| Sample fee fully credited on bulk order | Current written commercial policy and exceptions | Unverified in this repository |

Before release, also replace illustrative factory/project visuals with dated, rights-cleared originals where a photograph is presented as company or client evidence. Keep SKU-specific test reports, price, MOQ, lead time and destination compliance out of the company-level promise; provide them with the relevant quotation or technical pack.

Once approved, export a dated PDF and publish it alongside a short HTML summary with a clear inquiry route. Recheck all contact details and PDF links on the public site after deployment.
